**Antwan Gruber** is one of the protagonists of the first chapter of [[Lies and Deceit]]. He's the son of [[Judith-Noelle Gruber|Judith-Noelle]] and [[Tom Gruber|Tom]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[F.1.2.18.png]] 
> Antwan tells his siblings to be kind to their mom.

The youngest of the four Gruber siblings, Antwan grew up coddled by everyone in his family. [[Priscilla Gruber|Priscilla]] teased him less, [[Spenser Gruber|Spenser]] was kinder to him than others, and even [[Tom Gruber|Tom]] went out of his way to spend time with him. While this secretly bothered him, it gave Antwan to get to know his entire family on a personal level, something no one else had. When he did speak up, his words carried weight, and his suggestions were consistently sound.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[F.1.5.17.png]] 
> Antwan questions Priscilla and Daniel about his outfit.

Despite his unique position in the family, Antwan wanted to break out of his mold. His first opportunity was a night out at [[The Vault]], his first time getting to go to a club. Unfortunately for Antwan, [[Daniel Gruber|Daniel]] and Priscilla set him up by putting together an outfit ill suited for the occasion. While [[Judith-Noelle Gruber|Judith-Noelle]] comforted him and the twins apologized with a drink, Antwan chose to go home early, where he overheard Tom and their landlord, [[Ervin Stuart]], discussing the deal he gives Tom on the rent. That night, Antwan gained a better understanding of his father and just how precarious their financial situation was.

## Personality
As the youngest of the four Gruber siblings, Antwan is always babied by his siblings and parents. It's something that bothers Antwan, but this treatment lets him get through to all of his family. He's a voice of reason, an excellent listener, and someone the whole family knows is truthful. He cares deeply for his family in turn, and will always be the first to try to help out where he can.
