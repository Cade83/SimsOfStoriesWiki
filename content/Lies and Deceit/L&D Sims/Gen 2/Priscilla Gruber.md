**Priscilla Gruber** is one of the protagonists of the first chapter of [[Lies and Deceit]]. She's the twin sister of [[Daniel Gruber|Daniel]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[F.1.4.2.png]] 
> Priscilla pokes fun at Spenser.

Priscilla is the second oldest of the Gruber siblings by a single minute, just barely surpassed by [[Daniel Gruber|Daniel]]. She was always okay with that, as being the eldest sibling seemed to come with a certain degree of responsibility that Priscilla had no interest in taking on. Once [[Judith-Noelle Gruber|Judith-Noelle]] lost the election, Priscilla began to lash out at her siblings, relishing the thrill that came with being vaguely insulting or argumentative. That didn't mean that Priscilla was entirely heartless, however, as she pivots and offers comfort alongside Daniel when needed.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[F.1.3.24.png]] 
> Priscilla rants about their luck finding wealthy men to Daniel.

When Priscilla and Daniel finished school, they both chose not to go onto university. They felt that getting jobs that brought in money would help out their struggling family more than either of them furthering their eduction, and Priscilla wanted new clothes and treats. Priscilla found a job at a local clothing store and her income wasn't anything near what she'd envisioned. After a discussion with Daniel, the two of them chose to spend some of their free time on dating apps or roaming the grounds of the university, looking for rich men who could support them financially.

## Personality
While Priscilla is one of the two oldest siblings in the Gruber family, she's always refused the responsibility that comes with her age. She enjoys teasing her siblings and being unabashedly herself. Unlike her twin brother, [[Daniel Gruber|Daniel]], having to be the responsible oldest sibling was ill-opposed to how she wants to live out her life.

Priscilla is nearly inseparable from Daniel, and the pair of them do almost everything together. Any secrets the two of them have are shared between each other, and Priscilla's rather bold approach to life and vulgarity are complimented by Daniel's dry and sarcastic sense of humor.
