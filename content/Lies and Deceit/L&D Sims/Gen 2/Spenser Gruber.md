**Spenser Gruber** is one of the protagonists of the first chapter of [[Lies and Deceit]]. He's the son of [[Judith-Noelle Gruber|Judith-Noelle]] and [[Tom Gruber|Tom]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[F.1.2.17.png]] 
> Spenser with with his siblings.

The middle child of the Gruber children, stuck between the twins, [[Daniel Gruber|Daniel]] and [[Priscilla Gruber|Priscilla]] and his younger brother, [[Antwan Gruber|Antwan]], Spenser came to believe that he was destined to raise his family from the poverty they'd found themselves in. He recognized and believed that while [[Judith-Noelle Gruber|Judith-Noelle]] tried her best, [[Tom Gruber|Tom]] was near useless, the twins weren't much better, and Antwan, while well-intentioned, was far too timid to bring about meaningful change. He dedicated himself to his studies and external education all in pursuit of bettering his family.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[F.1.3.15.png]] 
> Spenser meets Naomi at the seminar.

Spenser's first big break came about when he attended a business seminar hosted by [[Sara Dishman]], a woman who'd pioneered a successful wellness brand. He intended on using the experience to both further his own knowledge and network using the lie that he was an experienced public relations manager from the respected Gruber family. His family name had no bearing on anything, but his 'experience' attracted the attention of [[Naomi Foye]], a fellow attendee looking to further her career in fashion. Spenser hit it off with Naomi, and the two of them exchanged contact info, mutually interested in pursuing a business partnership.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[F.1.4.19.png]] 
> Spenser preaches about marketing to Naomi.

When he met Naomi at [[Pepper’s Pub]], Spenser put his best foot forward, truly playing up the idea that he'd worked in PR. After ordering a drink, however, Spenser got tipsy and felt as if he had utterly humiliated himself and ruined what he'd set in motion. Once he returned home, he ignored Naomi's texts and pretended as if their meeting had never happened. A chance encounter at [[The Vault]] changed things, as Naomi directly confronted Spenser and convinced him that his inability to handle a drink hadn't ruined things between them.

## Personality
Spenser grew up seeing the consequences of [[Judith-Noelle Gruber|Judith-Noelle]]'s political failings on his family, and swore to himself that he would singlehandedly raise his family from poverty through whatever means necessary. He's not above lying to achieve his goals, and he does so to convince [[Naomi Foye|Naomi]] to go into business with him as a 'PR Manager'. Evidently, Spenser has a bit of an ego, and expects his family name to carry wait that it simply does not. He lacks confidence in the capabilities of his siblings and his parents, which is why he put saving his family on himself.

His ego and lofty expectations for himself mean that Spenser cannot tolerate his own failures. When he fails, Spenser rages and chooses to try to forget it ever happened- he refuses to let him humiliating himself be part of his story. [[Priscilla Gruber|Priscilla]], uniquely, refuses to let Spenser forget his mistakes which frustrates him to no end.
