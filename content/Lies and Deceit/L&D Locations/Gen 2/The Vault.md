**The Vault** is a location that appears in [[Lies and Deceit]].

## Profile
The Vault is an underground nightclub located in downtown [[Britechester]]. Fittingly named the Vault, its hidden behind a vault-lake door inside a building in Britechester's old town. It's known for its extensive dance floor, and on nights the local university teams compete, the club holds sports betting nights.

[[Ervin Stuart]], who is good with the owner of the Vault, is regularly able to secure free admission for himself and anyone he chooses to bring with him, typically [[Tom Gruber]]. He also takes the Gruber family here on Tom's suggestion to celebrate [[Judith-Noelle Gruber]]'s promotion at work.
