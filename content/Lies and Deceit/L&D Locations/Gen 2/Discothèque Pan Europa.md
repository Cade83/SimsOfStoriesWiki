**Discothèque Pan Europa** is a location introduced in [[Lies and Deceit]].

## Profile
Discothèque Pan Europa is a high-end nightclub located in downtown [[Windenburg]]. The cover price for getting in is typically steep, yet space is limited and the space almost always fills up. The club employs a handful of local DJs with famous headliners on their biggest nights, such as Fridays, Saturdays, and holidays.

[[Judith-Noelle Gruber]]'s campaign team rented Discothèque Pan Europa to celebrate her presidential campaign while they waited for the results to come in.
