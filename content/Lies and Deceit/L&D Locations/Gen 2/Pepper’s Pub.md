**Pepper’s Pub** is a location that appears in [[Lies and Deceit]].

## Profile
Pepper’s Pub is a dimly bit bar in [[Britechester]]'s old town. It has a long history dating back years, and has served local university students for as long as its existed. It's very beloved in its community, even serving locally brewed drinks, but to those unfamiliar with its history, it can seem a bit rundown.

[[Spenser Gruber]] and [[Naomi Foye]] meet up here to discuss a potential business partnership.
