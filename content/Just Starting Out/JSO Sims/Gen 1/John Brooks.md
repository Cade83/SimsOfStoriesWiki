**John Brooks** is one of the protagonists of the first chapter of [[Just Starting Out]]. He's the brother of [[Kayla Brooks]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.1.4.png]] 
> John asks Adrienne about their housing options.

John was born into and raised in a loving [[Adrienne and Jameson’s|home]] by his parents, [[Adrienne Brooks|Adrienne]] and [[Jameson Brooks|Jameson]], alongside his sister, [[Kayla Brooks|Kayla]]. While Adrienne was frequently busy at work, Jameson would regularly from home to ensure both John and Kayla received plenty of attention growing up. After graduating college, John took on a remote job working as a programmer for Landgraab Industries. Once John turned 25, Adrienne and Jameson made the decision to force their kids out of their home, and, with the aid of [[Adrienne's Real Estate]], John and Kayla purchased a [[Brooks House|home]] in [[Newcrest]].

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.5.7.png]] 
> John and Kayla meet with Malcolm at Magnolia Blossom.

They gradually built up their own lives, but struggled with the mundanity of the day-to-day. Still, John brought in more money than Kayla, so he devoted himself entirely to work, which led to him being promoted to head programmer of his organization, working directly under [[Nancy Landgraab]]. Kayla, on the other hand, worked on improving her life outlook. This led to her coming up with *Living on Less*, a lifestyle book series Kayla was so confident in that she took John on a trip to [[Willow Creek]]. While on this trip, John met [[Malcolm Landgraab]], Nancy's son and heir. Malcolm warned John that he'd be his future boss, so John chose to treat him with kindness and show him his favorite part of [[Magnolia Blossom]] park, impressing Malcolm.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.7.16.png]] 
> John chats with Cassandra.

When Kayla hosted the publication party for her first book, John invited [[Lillian Vickers]], a coworker of his, who he really clicked with and Kayla recommended he invite to more things. John had to put that thought on pause, however, when John volunteered to collect money owned to Nancy by the Goth family. While in town, he stopped by [[Municipal Muses]], where he encountered [[Cassandra Goth]] reading in the garden. After chatting, Cassandra agreed to introduce him to her parents so John could do as Nancy asked. John and Cassandra really bonded, and the two of them began regularly communicating over email.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.10.7 edited.png]] 
> Malcolm drives John around Oasis Springs.

Upon his return home, Kayla told John that Malcolm had attended a book signing she'd held to escape his parents and in hopes of seeing John. Knowing well how cruel Nancy was now that he was working under her, John chose to visit Malcolm in [[Oasis Springs]] to make sure he wasn't being abused. While at the [[Landgraab Mansion]], Nancy informed John that he'd impressed her. Malcolm eventually rescued John from her, and unaware of Malcolm's blatantly obvious feelings for him, John spent the day with him. After several drinks, Malcolm tucked a very drunk John into bed and admitted his feelings to him.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.14.4.png]] 
> John tells off Malcolm.

The following morning, John, believing that Malcolm had sexually assaulted him, stormed out of his room and walked in on Nancy and her husband, [[Geoffrey Landgraab|Geoffrey]], talking about marrying off Malcolm to a woman. It quickly became clear to him why exactly Malcolm wanted to get away from his parents. John chose to keep Malcolm's secret, even from Kayla, partially due to him not fully understanding and not wanting to discuss what happened that night. To his horror, Malcolm followed him home. John spoke with Malcolm privately and told him he'd keep his secret, but he needed to figure himself out and stay away.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.16.9.png]] 
> John goes on a date with Lillian.

John attended Kayla's next book signing and was able to quickly recognize that his sister had made it in the world. Among those in attendance were Cassandra, her little brother, [[Alexander Goth|Alexander]], and Lillian. Lillian inserted herself between John and Cassandra and insisted that he take her on a date, which John gave into. She took him to [[Baker and Barkley’s]], where she admitted that she'd been into him for a long time. At the end of the date, John asked Lillian to be his girlfriend.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.17.10.png]] 
> Kayla talks with John about Lillian and Malcolm.

As years passed, Kayla's renown continued to increase, bringing in wealth that far eclipsed John's, who had previously brought in most of the money. Lillian pushed John to do more with their wealth, but he refused, not wanting to become like the Landgraabs. Kayla, on the other hand, pushed John to propose to Lillian, but that conversation was cut short by Malcolm, who, following his parents' divorce, showed up on John's doorstep, as he had no other options. As he'd never explained his issues with Malcolm to Kayla, he had no retort when Kayla told him he could stay. In a private conversation with Malcolm, John told him that it was too late for him to explain things to Kayla, so he could stay as long as he kept his distance and their secret.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.23.8.png]] 
> John and Malcolm have a heart-to-heart.

John had little time to process Malcolm's new presence in his life, as he received an urgent email from Cassandra asking for help looking into her parents, [[Bella Goth|Bella]] and [[Mortimer Goth|Mortimer]], who were acting odd. Kayla and Malcolm accompanied him, with Lillian refusing due to the presence of Malcolm. Cassandra set John and Malcolm up at the [[Treefall Inn]], putting them in separate rooms at John's request. This forced John and Malcolm to have a serious discussion where Malcolm admitted that he'd been a brat and owed the Brooks a lot for all they'd done for him.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.25.36.png]] 
> John and Lillian breakup with each other.

Kayla and Cassandra did much of the investigation on their own and forced Bella to admit to the full truth. Following this reveal, Cassandra and Mortimer chose to move out immediately, and, with nowhere left to go, they moved in with John and Kayla. Shortly after getting back in town, John took Lillian on a date, whose self-absorbed tendencies and dislike for the Landgraab family were becoming an issue in their relationship, especially with Malcolm was present. When Lillian saw that Cassandra had moved in, she blew up out of jealousy, then she and John broke up through a very loud and public argument.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.26.22.png]] 
> Cassandra tells John about her goals.

When Cassandra uncovered a conspiracy involving her boss, President [[Mitch Laurent]], John agreed to accompany her to investigate Mitch's [[Laurent Windenburg Mansion|mansion]] in [[Windenburg]]. While on this trip, John got to learn a lot more about Cassandra's motivations and goals, understanding that she wanted to run for president to put an end to Mitch's corruption.

## Personality
John is, for the most part, a relatively passive man who hates to create problems for others. He's so passive that it has become an issue for him at multiple points in his life, with him only moving out of [[Adrienne Brooks|Adrienne]] and [[Jameson Brooks|Jameson]]'s house when forced, or him dating [[Lillian Vickers|Lillian]] for five years and never taking things further despite Lillian giving every indication that she wanted him to propose.

When pissed off or threatened, however, an entirely new John is unlocked. He's far from afraid of raising his voice or directly confronting someone about the issues he has with them. Lillian's frustration with [[Cassandra Goth|Cassandra]] unlocked the torrent of long-running issues he had with her, and John's long and complicated relationship with [[Malcolm Landgraab|Malcolm]] has involved him telling Malcolm off and demanding he keep his distance on many occasions.

John is ruled by his empathy, which plays a role in his passivity. He's let others dictate what happens in his life if the other person might benefit, such as when he focused on boring work so [[Kayla Brooks|Kayla]] could work towards her dreams, or his refusal to out Malcolm to [[Nancy Landgraab|Nancy]] despite his dislike for the man.
