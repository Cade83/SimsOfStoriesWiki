**Kayla Brooks** is one of the protagonists of the first chapter of [[Just Starting Out]]. She's the sister of [[John Brooks]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.1.2.png]] 
> Adrienne talks to Kayla about moving out.

Kayla was born into and raised in a loving [[Adrienne and Jameson’s|home]] by her parents, [[Adrienne Brooks|Adrienne]] and [[Jameson Brooks|Jameson]], alongside her brother, [[John Brooks|John]]. While Adrienne was frequently busy at work, Jameson would regularly from home to ensure both Kayla and John received plenty of attention growing up. Throughout and immediately after college, Kayla worked remotely as an editor. Once John turned 25, Adrienne and Jameson made the decision to force their kids out of their home. Kayla chose to accompany him, and, with the aid of [[Adrienne's Real Estate]], John and Kayla purchased a [[Brooks House|home]] in [[Newcrest]].

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.3.7.png]] 
> Kayla comes up with *Living on Less*.

They gradually built up their own lives, but struggled with the mundanity of the day-to-day and working remotely. Kayla looked to expand into creative writing to make life more interesting for herself, but she wasn't good at it and it did nothing to resolve the loneliness. After receiving some advice from Adrienne about putting herself out there, Kayla signed up for [[Melody Roach]]'s [[Ms. Roach’s Yoga|yoga class]], which wasn't cheap, and she didn't make much as an editor. During a session, Kayla had an epiphany where she came up with a series of lifestyle books she could write that discussed organizing one's life and mind and surrounding oneself with positivity to achieve inner peace. She felt like it was something she was qualified to write, as that way of living was something she'd tried to practice since moving.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.6.12.png]] 
> Kayla thanks Melody at her publication party.

After working on the first entry of her book series, titled *Living on Less*, Kayla was so confident in her work that she took John on a trip to [[Willow Creek]] to see their parents and go out drinking. While out at the bar, she and John met [[Malcolm Landgraab]], who would one day be John's future boss. Malcolm was excessively rude, which Kayla didn't appreciate, and she was sure to make that clear to both Malcolm himself and John. Upon publishing her first book, Kayla hosted a party where she was sure to celebrate and thank Melody for being her initial inspiration, letting her know that she'd put her business in the acknowledgements.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.8.8.png]] 
> Kayla hosts her first book signing.

Kayla had her first ever book signing at the [[Newcrest Public Library]], which, to Kayla's horror, had poor attendance. She had, however, developed one devoted fan, a woman named [[Mila Munch]]. It was also attended by Malcolm, who went in hopes of seeing John, leading to Kayla beginning to suspect he was in love with her brother. Going against Kayla's advice, John chose to go visit Malcolm personally out of concern for him. While John was away, Kayla was accosted by Mila at [[Newcrest Groceries]], who wanted live advice and secrets. Freaked out, Kayla ran to Melody, and she helped her through her stress related to John and Malcolm, as well as her now having an obnoxious fan.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.15.6.png]] 
> Kayla sees the crowd outside for her second book signing.

With newfound clarity, Kayla finished and published her second book, which was a much greater success than the first. The library invited her to host another book signing, and, to Kayla's shock, the library was packed. Feeling much more confident in herself, she thanked Mila, who was in attendance again, for being her first fan. Melody, who was also in attendance, thanked Kayla for promoting her business and inspiring her to become a therapist by coming to her for guidance. Kayla then found the [[Librarian|librarian]] and arranged things so she could speak to everyone outside, where there was room. In that moment, Kayla knew she'd made it.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.18.5 V2.png]] 
> Kayla and John talk with Malcolm.

As years passed, Kayla's renown and fame continued to increase, bringing in attention and wealth that encouraged her and John to improve the house and put up privacy hedges. Kayla pushed John to propose to his longtime girlfriend, [[Lillian Vickers]], knowing that she was growing concerned. However, their conversation was interrupted by Malcolm, who had run away from home and needed a place to stay. Feeling sympathetic for the man, Kayla offered to let him stay as long as he needed as long as he let his parents know he was okay.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.21.31.png]] 
> Kayla bonds with Cassandra.

When John's friend, [[Cassandra Goth]], asking for assistance with looking into her parents [[Bella Goth|Bella]] and [[Mortimer Goth|Mortimer]], Kayla and Malcolm agreed to accompany John to Willow Creek. Kayla went to visit Adrienne and Jameson, as well as Melody, who had moved to Willow Creek to open her [[Melody's Therapy|therapy practice]]. While in town, Kayla stayed with Cassandra at the [[Goth Mansion]] and learned a lot about the Goth family and Cassandra through both discussion and snooping. After a discussion with Melody, who happened to be the Goth family therapist now, Melody concluded that Bella and Mortimer were becoming vampires.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.24.4.png]] 
> Kayla tells Cassandra about Melody's deduction.

Kayla wasn't confident in Melody's deduction and had a letter on hand she'd stolen from the master bedroom. After showing it to Cassandra, Cassandra went to confront Bella, being sure to involve her younger brother [[Alexander Goth|Alexander]] in the discussion. Bella's story didn't add up to Kayla, and she finally told her what Melody has concluded. While talking, they watched as [[Vladislaus Straud]], a blatant vampire, attacked John and Malcolm. This revelation forced Bella to spill the full truth.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.26.34.png]] 
> Kayla tells Malcolm about her improved opinion of him.

Furious at their parents, Cassandra and Mortimer chose to move out immediately, and, with nowhere left to go, they moved in with Kayla and John. Kayla hired an architect to work on adding a second floor to the house for the Goths. Shortly after everyone moved in, Kayla chose to accompany Malcolm to see his long-lost brother, [[Johnny Zest]]. Hearing about Malcolm's previous home life from Johnny, Kayla, near tears, begged Malcolm to talk to her about things like this in the future and told him that he'd improved as a person since their first meeting.

## Personality
Kayla is a very outspoken woman, tending to freely speak her mind without fear of repercussions. She told [[Malcolm Landgraab|Malcolm]] exactly what she thought of him and his behavior upon their second meeting, and was more than willing to meet [[Adrienne Brooks|Adrienne]] and [[Jameson Brooks|Jameson]] exactly where they were at when they made dirty jokes. Kayla has so little filter that it worried [[Melody Roach|Melody]] when they were in public. 

Her outspokenness isn't relegated to just her willingness to call people out of having a dirty mouth, but also includes her willingness to have very real conversations with others. On multiple occasions, she sat [[John Brooks|John]] down and talked to him about his passivity. This trait of Kayla's also aided her in quickly building deep bonds with people like Melody, [[Cassandra Goth|Cassandra]], and Malcolm, who appreciated her authentic candor. 

Kayla's ability to connect also comes from how introspective she is. While it wasn't a skill she had at first, she's incredibly intelligent and skilled at taking a step back and analyzing an entire situation. She figured out Malcolm's crush on John very early on, could read when [[Bella Goth|Bella]] told a half-truth to Cassandra, and, most importantly, knows how to read into and encourage others to achieve inner peace, as reflected in *Living in Less*. Kayla never lets her fame get to her head, as it would shatter the inner peace she built for herself.
