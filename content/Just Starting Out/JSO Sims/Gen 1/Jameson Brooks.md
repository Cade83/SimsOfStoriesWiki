**Jameson Brooks** is a character in the first chapter of [[Just Starting Out]]. He's the father of [[John Brooks|John]] and [[Kayla Brooks]].

## Profile
Jameson was born in [[Willow Creek]] and grew up immersed in the social world of the area. He was decently popular in school, and after graduation, he opened his own car repair shop. Jameson's social connections brought plenty of customers in, and as he grew more skilled, he broadened his skillset, becoming a general mechanic. He also wanted to get more in shape, so he started going to the gym, where he saw a flyer advertising a yoga class. Jameson signed up on a whim and met [[Adrienne Brooks|Adrienne]] at his first class.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.1.1.png]] 
>  Adrienne and Jameson push their children to move out.

Jameson and Adrienne fell in love, got married, had [[John Brooks|John]], then [[Adrienne and Jameson’s|moved in together]]. Once [[Kayla Brooks|Kayla]] was born, Adrienne went off to take real estate classes, and Jameson became a borderline stay-at-home dad for a bit. He continued to go to the shop when he could, but when Adrienne was busy all day, he's work on smaller repair jobs in the family garage. Jameson and Adrienne continued to raise John and Kayla with love for many years, but once John turned 25, they both mutually agreed that it was time for two of them to hit the road.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.4.8.png]] 
>  Jameson warns John about the Landgraabs.

Adrienne located a house for their kids in [[Newcrest]], but neither Jameson or Adrienne had any interest in letting them live out their lives without them. They made a habit of dropping in on them by surprise  to offer their guidance, company, and emotional support. Upon hearing that John had taken on a job at Landgraab Industries, Jameson warned him about the danger of the Landgraab family. John and Kayla also continued to visit them in Willow Creek as well, but their visits were more planned, often part of a larger trip to the area. At around this time, Jameson took on a secondary job working as a docent at [[Municipal Muses]].

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.14.1.png]] 
>  Jameson questions Malcolm.

Time continued to pass, and John and Kayla got much busier in their own personal life as they matured, meaning that their visits became less frequent, and their parents' advice less welcome. Regardless, their kids kept in touch and kept them updated about their day-to-day lives, and Jameson and Adrienne continued to drop in, just less frequently. In one surprise visit, Jameson personally confronted [[Malcolm Landgraab]] about his family's rumored illicit dealings on his son's behalf, forcing Malcolm to swear to protect John. During this time in their life, Jameson and Adrienne also began traveling extensively, and they were always sure to drop by whenever their travels took them by Newcrest. 

## Personality
Jameson's easygoing personality and generally relaxed vibe make him endearing to almost everyone who has the pleasure of meeting him. His repair shop thrived because of a combination of his personality and his skill, and Jameson made an excellent museum docent because of his ability to put others at ease. [[Adrienne Brooks|Adrienne]] fell for him after their first yoga class, and the two of them spent many happy years together after. Jameson always let Adrienne be the one to pressure [[John Brooks|John]] and [[Kayla Brooks|Kayla]] to move out, but he'd always back her up. 

Supporting Adrienne and his kids is very important to Jameson. John's job with the Landgraabs always worried Jameson, as he's heard nasty rumors about that family. So, when given the chance to confront [[Malcolm Landgraab|Malcolm]], Jameson dropped his usual easygoing persona to question him directly, determined to find out if his son was safe or not.
