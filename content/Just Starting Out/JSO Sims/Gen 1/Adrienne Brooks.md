**Adrienne Brooks** is a character in the first chapter of [[Just Starting Out]]. She's the mother of [[John Brooks|John]] and [[Kayla Brooks]], and a [[Adrienne's Real Estate|realtor]] who serves the [[Willow Creek]] area.

## Profile
Adrienne moved to [[Willow Creek]] when she was still young, knowing no one in the area. She had a lonely start, working a series of jobs she hated with nothing social to look forward to. Eventually, she started going to the gym, and at one point, she saw a flyer advertising a yoga club. Adrienne chose to attend, craving social stimulation, and it was at that club where she met [[Jameson Brooks|Jameson]] and most of her adult friends.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.1.1.png]] 
>  Adrienne and Jameson push their children to move out.

Adrienne and Jameson fell in love, got married, then had [[John Brooks|John]] and [[Adrienne and Jameson’s|moved in together]]. Once Adrienne had [[Kayla Brooks|Kayla]], she signed up for classes to secure a real estate license. Within a year, she was realtor that served the Willow Creek area. Adrienne and Jameson raised John and Kayla with love for many years, but once John turned 25, they both mutually agreed that it was time for two of them to hit the road, and they began to push constantly.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.1.3.png]] 
>  Adrienne locates a house for her kids.

When John and Kayla finally gave in, Adrienne helped them find a [[Brooks House|home]], meeting with the two of them in her [[Adrienne's Real Estate|office]]. Their only option was near a [[BFFs House|house]] she'd recently sold to some teenagers Adrienne hadn't particularly liked, so she broadened her search and eventually located a place in [[Newcrest]]. John and Kayla took it, trusting Adrienne's judgement.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[G.1.5.4.png]] 
>  John and Kayla visit their parents.

As John and Kayla got accustomed to their new life, Adrienne and Jameson would regularly drop in on them, often as a surprise, to offer their guidance, company, and emotional support. Adrienne personally pushed Kayla into signing up for [[Ms. Roach’s Yoga]], which proved to be life changing for her. John and Kayla continued to visit them in Willow Creek as well, but their visits were more planned, often part of a larger trip to the area.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[G.1.13.3.png]] 
>  Adrienne and Jameson surprise John.

Time continued to pass, and John and Kayla got much busier in their own personal life as they matured, meaning that their visits became less frequent, and their parents' advice less welcome. Regardless, their kids kept in touch and kept them updated about their day-to-day lives, and Adrienne and Jameson continued to drop in, just less frequently. During this time in their life, Adrienne and Jameson also began traveling extensively, and they were always sure to drop by whenever their travels took them by Newcrest. 

## Personality
Adrienne is a very gregarious woman with little filter. She proudly says exactly what's on her mind, even if its in the form of an innuendo, as she likes to have a bit of fun with what she says. However, she's so pleasant-natured and easy to talk to that her lack of a filter helps her more than it hurts her, as professionally, her clients know that she's being upfront and honest with them. 

Adrienne adores her family, but 25 years with her kids was pushing it for both her and [[Jameson Brooks|Jameson]]. She always had a desire to seem interesting, even lying about her love story with [[Jameson Brooks|Jameson]] to [[John Brooks|John]] and [[Kayla Brooks|Kayla]] to spice things up a little. With them gone, Adrienne and Jameson were able to live more wildly, but at the same time, they'd still drop anything to soar to [[Newcrest]] and help their kids out.
