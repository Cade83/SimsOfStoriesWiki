The **Newcrest Public Library** is a library in [[Newcrest]].

## Profile
The Newcrest Public Library was built to look like a classical building, and, located near [[Newcrest]]'s town center, serves much of Newcrest's population. As a fixture in its community, the library works to uplift local authors and regularly gives used books to those in need.

One of these authors was [[Kayla Brooks]], with the library agreeing to host a book signing for her after the publication of her first book in the *Living on Less* series. While her first signing had poor turnout, Kayla was still invited back for a second, which had such a massive turnout that it overwhelmed the library and its [[Librarian|librarian]], requiring Kayla to move things outside.
