**Municipal Muses** is a museum in [[Willow Creek]].

## Profile
Municipal Muses is a museum on [[Willow Creek]]'s main street that recounts the history and culture of the area for visitors. A free museum, it is funded and owned by the Goth family, and was frequented by [[Cassandra Goth]] in her youth as a result.

[[Jameson Brooks]] took on a job as a docent here after his kids moved out, teaching guests about the town he'd lived in his entire life.
