**Max Villareal** is a character in the first chapter of [[Reborn from the Ash]]. He's the youngest heir to the Villareal fortune and the leader of the [[Renegades]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[K.1.3.40.png]] 
> Max spends time with Danna.

As the youngest of the three Villareal siblings, Max grew up completely free of his father's, [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]]'s, expectations for the future heir of his company and [[Villareal Estate|estate]]. Feeling rather left out of things, Max would often cruelly lash out at others, which only pushed him further from his father and older siblings [[Hugo Villareal|Hugo]] and [[Luna Villareal|Luna]]. The only family member he connected with was his mother, [[Danna Villareal|Danna]], who was similarly excluded from family gatherings.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.8.16.png]] 
> The Renegades form.

When Danna vanished under mysterious circumstances, Max was distraught. He wanted to inflict his rage upon others, but Jacques certainly wasn't going to tolerate it in his home. Max ended up at the [[Bathe de Rill]], where he got a kick out of graffitiing the bathroom. That same day, he encountered [[Ulrike Faust]],  [[Wolfgang Munch]], and [[Morgan Fyres]], and after getting to know each other, the four of them formed the [[Renegades]], a group of outcasts dedicated to making things bad for other people.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[K.1.8.17.png]] 
> A Renegades meeting.

Max became much closer to the Renegades than to his own family. Once Jacques passed and Hugo inherited the family business and estate, he gave Max free reign to do whatever he wished with the basement. Max transformed it into a space for the Renegades to hang out, as they'd become somewhat notorious and weren't welcome anywhere. This basement saw the departure of Wolfgang from their group and the addition of [[Ivan Dishman]] and [[Ronnie Mai]]. The motto of the Renegades was that they weren't there to make friends, but Max came to value each member- Wolfgang's departure wounded him deeply, so he branded him a traitor.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.7.13.png]] 
> Max makes a deal with Antonio.

Throughout the Renegades existence, Max had financed them almost entirely himself with his own inheritance. When [[Antonio Sanchez]] paid them a visit with a job that would pay, Max knew he was going to accept. He didn’t trust Antonio, but he wanted to secure enough to keep his people more than comfortable. He agreed to Antonio’s terms, then as planned, he and Morgan kidnapped [[Jany Marks|Jany]] and [[Skylar Marks]] and left a threatening note in their apartment to ensure that [[Josh Marks]] would have no choice but to meet with Antonio and [[Jacqueline Sanchez]].

## Personality
Max has a tendency to be cruel and outspoken, delighting in saying the harshest things on his mind. His whole world view is incredibly pessimistic, shaped by his relationship with his family. [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]] never valued him, [[Hugo Villareal|Hugo]] and [[Luna Villareal|Luna]] were included in things, while he and [[Danna Villareal|Danna]] were left out. Seeing that they’d never prioritized him, he came to the realization that he needed to prioritize himself above all others. This led to Max’s bold personality and rather shrewd and astute approach to things- anything he did needed to benefit him personally. 

However, the few that Max was able to connect with became people he valued highly. He himself isn’t sure if it’s because the [[Renegades]] serve some personal value to him or if he just wants to improve their lives, but he dedicates himself to making their lives as comfortable as possible, as he has the means to do so. His deal with [[Antonio Sanchez|Antonio]] came at a perfect time- he obviously didn’t trust the man, but it was an opportunity to improve the situation for everyone he cared about and himself personally at the expense of people he neither knew or valued.
