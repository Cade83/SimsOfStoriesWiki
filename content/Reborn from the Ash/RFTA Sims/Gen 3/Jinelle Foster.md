**Jinelle Foster** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Reborn from the Ash]]. She's the personal servant of [[Jacqueline Sanchez]].

## Profile
Jinelle first encountered [[Jacqueline Sanchez]] when working as a low-ranking journalist at a fashion show. The two of them got to talking, and by the end of the night, Jacqueline offered Jinelle a position at [[Sanchez Mansion|her mansion]]. Jinelle, who hated her job, happily accepted. Thanks to Jacqueline, Jinelle got to skip [[Ivailo Rodrigues]]'s, the head servant's, typical interview and screening, but she still ended up doing the most basic of cleaning. She quietly did her duties, keeping to herself and regretting her life choices. When Jinelle eventually vocalized this, it was clear that Ivailo was furious with her, but Jacqueline swooped in and ensured that Jinelle kept her job. With Jacqueline's aid, Jinelle swiftly climbed the ladder and soon became Jacqueline's personal servant. This came with access to a wardrobe full of outfits Jacqueline had retired, with Jacqueline requested she wear, so they both looked fashionable at all times. With that, Jinelle was happy.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.6.4.png]] 
> Jinelle accompanies Jacqueline to town.

As Jacqueline's personal servant, Jinelle was free to speak her mind, and anyone who dared criticize her questionable work ethic was met with Jacqueline's wrath. She was de-facto the second in command servant, not that her work showed it. Jacqueline kept her busy, but the tasks she assigned were typically simple, such as fetching her daughter [[Kiara Sanchez|Kiara]], accompanying her to town, or putting on a new outfit. She did occasionally end up doing work for [[Antonio Sanchez|Antonio]], Jacqueline's husband, but it was always something Jacqueline signed off on first.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[K.1.8.3.png]] 
> Jinelle welcomes Josh to the Sanchez Mansion.

One of the tasks Jacqueline approved of involved the capture of [[Josh Marks]]. Jinelle was supposed to be more involved, but she ended up merely assisting Ivailo with welcoming Josh to the mansion, infuriating Jacqueline. For the first time ever, Jacqueline requested that Jinelle keep a secret from Antonio, which Jinelle agreed to without question.

## Personality
Jinelle approaches the entirety of life with a great deal of exasperation. There's never a task she doesn't see as pointless or a waste of time, and with [[Jacqueline Sanchez|Jacqueline]]'s protection, she makes those thoughts known. Jinelle lacks any filter and will unabashedly speak her mind. Despite her distaste for labor, Jinelle has a great deal of respect for Jacqueline and is willing to set aside her reservations to do what she asks, even if she complains along the way.

Jinelle's position grants her access to fancy clothing and respect from her peers, which is more than enough for her. She hates the head servant, [[Ivailo Rodrigues|Ivailo]], who she frequently has to tolerate due to both of them serving a head of the house, but she's willing to do it to look nice and to impress Jacqueline.
