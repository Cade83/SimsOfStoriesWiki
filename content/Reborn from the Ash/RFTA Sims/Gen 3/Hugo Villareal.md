**Hugo Villareal** is a character in the first chapter of [[Reborn from the Ash]]. He inherited the business of his father, [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]], and carries it on in his stead.

## Profile
Hugo is the oldest of the three Villareal children, and therefore grew up with the greatest expectations placed upon him. His father, [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]]. raised him to take over for him once he was gone, regularly pulling him aside to demonstrate how he did things or teach him valuable knowledge. His sister, [[Luna Villareal|Luna]], sometimes joined him, but it was clear to Hugo that she was merely the backup. When he wasn’t busy, Hugo found a great deal of joy in cooking for his family. It brought him comfort, and was a way to show how much he cared for them. Once his mother [[Danna Villareal|Danna]] mysteriously vanished, however, Jacques took that joy away from him, going as far as to speak with his school to modify his schedule so he was taking solely classes that would benefit him most  in the future with heavy workloads, so he had no more time for hobbies like cooking.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.3.6.png]] 
> Hugo at the [[Sanchez Mansion]] for a meeting with Antonio.

Once Jacques passed, Hugo was ushered into his role almost immediately. He was responsible for running the [[Villareal Estate|estate]], but he did his best to shift some of that work to Luna and his little brother, [[Max Villareal|Max]]. The most time consuming work was the business relationship Jacques had with [[Antonio Sanchez]]. Hugo had to start meeting with Antonio regularly, which always made him nervous, and become aware of handle various darker details of his father’s work than he hadn’t been previously aware of. Regardless of his own thoughts and feelings, Hugo shouldered the work and kept things running, as he’d been trained to do.

## Personality
Hugo feels deeply inadequate and ill-suited for the life he was given. He allowed himself to be bullied by [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]] throughout his childhood, was robbed of his passions and talents, and as an adult, he felt as if all he had left was the role Jacques had prepared himself for. So, as much as he feels he isn't meant to be working the job he does, Hugo dedicates himself to working alongside [[Antonio Sanchez|Antonio]] as his father had before him.
