**Luna Villareal** is a character in the first chapter of [[Reborn from the Ash]]. She's the daughter of [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]] and a longtime friend of [[Kiara Sanchez]].

## Profile
Luna was the second born child to the wealthy [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]] and [[Danna Villareal]]. She grew up in the opulence of the [[Villareal Estate]], living a privileged life, but from a young age, Luna and her older brother [[Hugo Villareal|Hugo]] were taught about the Villareal family values and business. Once Danna disappeared suddenly when Luna was still young, she retreated within herself, and Jacques shifted all of his attention to Hugo. With her newfound free time, Luna was able to spend a little more time hanging out with [[Kiara Sanchez]], the daughter of Jacques's business partner, [[Antonio Sanchez|Antonio]]. They became the closest of friends.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.3.10.png]] 
> Luna and Kiara begin to plot their escape.

Once Jacques passed and Hugo stepped into his father's position, Luna wanted to get out while she could, fearing Hugo becoming just as bad as their father. When venting about her fears to Kiara, who similarly disliked her own family, Kiara suggested that they both flee their lives. They didn't get far, however, as they were caught by [[Max Villareal|Max]], Luna's little brother, who quickly told Hugo and Antonio about what they were planning. After that day, Kiara wasn't allowed to spend nearly as much time with Luna, so she began to experiment with new ways to improve her life.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[K.1.6.27.png]] 
> Luna admits that she's glad Jacques is dead.

Luna tracked down plans Danna had drawn up to improve the estate's grounds and dedicated herself to implementing them. She also began to pursue fashion, studying what was trending and learning to create her own clothing line that she hoped she could one day market. Both Luna and Max distanced themselves from Hugo and everything he had to manage, and conversations between all three siblings became much less frequent. Luna felt that Jacques's expectations had ruined Hugo, and she hated Jacques for it. It wasn't a thought she vocalized until Kiara came to visit to complain about her mother, [[Jacqueline Sanchez|Jacqueline]], and ended up chatting with both Luna and Max. Max was both shocked and impressed, as he'd had the same thought for years.

## Personality
Luna tends to be on the quieter side, but she speaks with such earnestness that others are naturally drawn to her. She approaches life with a calm that she adopted after she'd properly processed [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]]'s death. After his passing, Luna did seem deep reflecting and came to understand her privilege, how little she had in common with [[Hugo Villareal|Hugo]] and [[Max Villareal|Max]], how much she despised Jacques, and what truly mattered to her- the little, beautiful things in life.

Unlike her actual family, Luna bonded with [[Kiara Sanchez|Kiara]] easily. They grew up in similar situations, and their relationship endured the test of time. While Kiara has a much narrower view of the world, Luna is always there to lend an open ear. Listening and providing sound advice is a strength of hers.
