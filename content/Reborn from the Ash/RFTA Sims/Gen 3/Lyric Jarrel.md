**Lyric Jarrel** is a character in the first chapter of [[Reborn from the Ash]]. She's a bodyguard employed by the Sanchez Family, and is the personal servant to and close confidant of [[Kiara Sanchez]].

## Profile
When Lyric was fresh out of school, had no real passions to pursue, but wanted to move out of her parents place, she happened to chance upon a posting in the paper for a live-in servant. Lyric showed up at the [[Sanchez Mansion]] at the time appointed in the paper listing, and met with [[Ivailo Rodrigues]], who interviewed her about her capabilities. While she got the job, she ended up posted in the kitchen, which Lyric was less than thrilled about, but the pay and housing was too good to quit. She was stuck there for a few months, serving food exactly as requested, until [[Kiara Sanchez]], the youngest Sanchez, absentmindedly asked Lyric if she could ignore the direction of her parents and get her the food she wanted. Bored, Lyric obliged, which led to a growing connection between the two of them, aided by their closeness in age, which [[Antonio Sanchez]] soon took note of. He put Lyric through combat training, and with time, he was satisfied enough with her performance to appoint her to the position of Kiara's personal servant and bodyguard.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.2.14.png]] 
> Lyric listens intently to one of Kiara's rants.

Kiara never had much for Lyric to do, so Lyric often defaulted to doing things for Antonio. This meant that she retained the relatively easy position of working for Kiara while continuing to gain Antonio's confidence, becoming privy to increasingly high-secrecy missions and information, often working alongside Ivailo, Antonio's personal servant. Kiara, naturally, always remained Lyric's top priority, and Lyric had to make herself available whenever Kiara had to vent. Not that she minded- as years passed, Lyric came to consider Kiara to be a friend.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[K.1.4.7.png]] 
> Lyric gives her best advice in response to Kiara.

Eventually, their closeness culminated in a rant from Kiara that Lyric has never expected- one where she acknowledged her privilege. When asked if she should run away from her cushy life, all Lyric could suggest was to do what would make her the happiest. Kiara chose to leave the Sanchez Mansion, which resulted in a new mission from Antonio- keep an eye on Kiara, switching out with Ivailo when needed, which would also ensure that her reports were accurate.

## Personality
Lyric is an incredibly level-headed woman, able to handle tasks of all kinds and endure less than pleasant situations as long as she feels well compensated. She's able to handle [[Kiara Sanchez|Kiara]], who regularly vented to her about anything on her mind when she lived in the [[Sanchez Mansion]], to the intense [[Antonio Sanchez|Antonio]], who demands unquestioning loyalty and dedication to any task given out. The only person who's able to knock her off guard is [[Ivailo Rodrigues|Ivailo]], who Lyric dislikes greatly.

Over her time with the Sanchez family, Lyric developed a closeness to Kiara, coming to see her as much more than the person she was serving. Antonio's demands to keep watch on her and report back was the first real conflict of interest she felt in her line of work, but in the end, she kept her head down and did as required.
