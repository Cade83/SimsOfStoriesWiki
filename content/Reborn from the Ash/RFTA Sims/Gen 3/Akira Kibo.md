**Akira Kibo** is a character in [[Mai Legacy]] and [[Reborn from the Ash]]. He's a close friend of [[Salim Benali]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.9.4 v2.png]] 
> Akira sharing his first kiss with Jacques.

Akira moved out of his parents' house the day he turned 18, determined to strike out on his own. He had meager savings and no plans beyond going out to the clubs with his longtime friends [[Susan Harper]] and [[Salim Benali]]. Salim was a friend from school, and Susan a neighbor a few years his senior. His first night out, he found himself dancing with the infamous [[Jacques Villareal]]. The two of them ran into each other several times, and soon struck up a romantic relationship, having their first kiss at the [[Stargazer Lounge]].

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.3.3 v2.png]] 
> Akira protests rent prices.

Jacques and Akira held their wedding at the same location within a few months of their first kiss. Jacques was getting older and Akira was borderline homeless, so they rushed into it, much to the media's scorn. Once Akira quit his job and moved into the [[Villareal Estate]], he and Jacques began to get into regular arguments over Salim, who Jacques felt Akira was too close to. Things culminated in Jacques temporarily throwing Akira out, forcing him to rent an apartment in [[San Myshuno]] near Susan. Jacques paid for it, but he often threatened to withhold Akira's rent money, driving Akira to lead a series of protests against the insane rent prices. It was at one of those protests where Akira met [[James Mai]].

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.5.6 v2.png]] 
> Akira watches Susan and James interact.

James fell for Susan hard and fast and frightened Susan off, so Akira stepped in to comfort him, as the two of them had hit it off and he felt bad for him. Recognizing that James had no friends, he introduced him to Salim. Still, as friends of both Susan and James, Akira wanted the two of them to make amends, so he planned for the two of them to run into each other at Geekcon so they were forced to interact. Akira's plan worked, and they began hanging out again.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.7.2 v2.png]] 
> Akira and Susan lock Jacques in James's house.

Jacques recognized Akira's growing closeness to James, and he was once again consumed by jealousy. He arranged for a meeting with James with Akira present where he threatened James, telling him to back off. This still wasn't enough for Jacques, and he attempted to rob [[Mai House|James's house]]. He was stopped by Akira and Susan and arrested when Akira called the police.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.9.1 v2.png]] 
> Akira sits beside Jacques's death bed.

After his arrest, Jacques's health began to decline. Akira kept going out and partying, as he typically did, as he wanted to hide the fact that Jacques was in poor health and that he was hurting. Jacques eventually passed due to heart failure, caused by years of stress and intense bursts of rage. Akira organized his funeral, which was held in the [[Villareal Mausoleum]]. With Jacques gone, Akira moved out of the Villareal Estate for good, as Jacques had left it to his children, and returned to his apartment. He got a job as a barista to make ends meet.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.18.11.png]] 
> Akira and Salim arrive at their wedding.

Akira tried his best to stay social, still attending James and Susan's baby shower, for instance, but he was hurting. He turned to Salim, as he was similarly lonely and one of his longest running friends. The two began to spend a significant amount of time together, and with time, Akira gradually began to move on. As he moved on, however, he began to recognize that he'd developed feelings for Salim. Once he admitted that to himself, things moved quickly, the two of them started dating, then Akira proposed. Akira and Salim had moved in silence, so the sudden announcement of their wedding came as a great surprise to everyone they knew. Their wedding was a flashy and extravagant affair, held at [[Myshuno Meadows]].

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.25.16.png]] 
> Akira and Salim kiss for the last time to humor Seth.

Akira and Salim spent several years together and gradually came to realize that they were nowhere near as compatible as they'd thought. Salim enjoyed his alone time, as it was when he was able to focus on writing the best, and Akira was prone to fits of intense affection that drove Salim insane. While at [[Leah Mai]]'s wedding, [[Penny Pizzazz]], a friend of Susan's, asked Akira and Salim if they were truly happy, and after a few days of reflection, they both realized they liked each other better as friends. They went and told Susan and James, but tried their best to hide their divorce from their son, [[Seth Mai|Seth]], who had idolized their relationship.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.8.27.png]] 
> Akira serves Kiara coffee.

As Akira's oldest friends, such as Susan and James, continued to age, he moved out of Salim's apartment and rented a new place in [[Windenburg]]. With his existing barista experience, he was able to secure a job at [[South Square Coffee]] which was enough for him to make ends meet. While working there, he encountered [[Kiara Sanchez]], and Akira was enchanted by her complete lack of knowledge about basic societal norms. He sat down with her, determined to get to the bottom of the mystery.

## Personality
Akira is a social butterfly who loves a party, and he spends much of his life in pursuit of the next social outing. He loves the chance to get to know a new person, and many of his most important relationships, such as those with [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]] and [[Kiara Sanchez|Kiara]], began as chance encounters he initiated. He has a bold and cheeky sense of humor that makes him immediately endearing, so connecting with others is easy for him.

Despite his ability to connect with others, Akira dislikes it when people feel bad for him. When Jacques's health turned, Akira hid it, and even continued going out and partying, even joining his friends at the [[Windenburg Ruins]]. Once Jacques passed, he nearly isolated himself as the sympathies and well-wishes of others were unavoidable. [[Salim Benali|Salim]] was the only one who understood what Akira needed on a personal level, as he offered him company without bringing up what Akira was trying to avoid.

Akira is also a man who falls hard and fast when it comes to love. [[Susan Harper|Susan]] says he falls in love with someone's personality and capacity for good things, not superficial things such as gender, looks, or wealth. In every instance where Akira fell for someone, he connected with that individual on a personal level and came to understand their soul, even if externally, they seem unpleasant. Akira knows what he likes, and is good at brushing off criticism about his relationships, having faced the media with Jacques and his friends with Salim.
