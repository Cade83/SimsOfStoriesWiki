**Ivailo Rodrigues** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Reborn from the Ash]]. He's dedicated his life to [[Antonio Sanchez]].

## Profile
Ivailo was orphaned from a young age. He hated the orphanage and ran off, finding work fishing at the [[Windenburg]] harbor. As a minor, he wasn't supposed to be working, so his employers didn't report his earnings and eventually began to garnish his wages for their own personal gain, forcing Ivailo to work more for pennies. At around this time, [[Antonio Sanchez]] began to reform shipping at the harbor, and he took note of Ivailo, who was quite obviously being taken advantage of. Antonio ensured that Ivailo got all of what he was owed, then offered him a position working for him. After a short while working for him, Antonio briefly returned home to [[Selvadorada]] and trusted Ivailo to keep things running in his absence by maintaining his working relationship with the [[Jacques Villareal|Villareal family]] and keeping the port operating as designed. Starstruck, when Antonio returned, Ivailo swore his undying loyalty to him.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.6.15.png]] 
> Ivailo berates Jinelle, Jacqueline's personal servant.

Once the [[Sanchez Mansion]] was built, Ivailo took on the position of head servant. He kept the house staff in order while continuing to take on various tasks as requested by Antonio, becoming privy to more of the company's going-ons than Antonio's wife, [[Jacqueline Sanchez|Jacqueline]]. When Antonio's daughter, [[Kiara Sanchez|Kiara]], left home, Antonio requested that [[Lyric Jarrel]], Kiara's personal servant, keep an eye on her while also secretly requesting that Ivailo keep an eye on Lyric by switching out watch duties with her on occasion.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[K.1.8.4.png]] 
> Ivailo welcomes [[Josh Marks]] to the mansion.

When Antonio and Jacqueline hatched a plan to bring Kiara back by finding her a husband they could manipulate, Ivailo helped Antonio rope in [[Max Villareal]] and the rest of the [[Renegades]], who he would continue to help Antonio manage. He arranged things so that once each potential suitor had been motivated to meet with Antonio and Jacqueline, he and [[Jinelle Foster]], Jacqueline's personal servant, would welcome them to the Sanchez Mansion and explain their aims. The suitor was then passed on to Jinelle and Jacqueline for a full makeover. Throughout this process, Ivailo continued to keep a watchful eye on Lyric and Kiara.

## Personality
Ivailo is entirely dedicated to [[Antonio Sanchez|Antonio]]. He views himself as Antonio's right hand, and carries out any demand of his without question, regardless of morality. Antonio saved Ivailo, gave him purpose, and Ivailo ensures that he lives up to every last expectation. Ivailo is rather cold towards others, especially the other servants, viewing [[Lyric Jarrel|Lyric]]'s flippant lack of concern for Kiara with disdain that he is happy to make apparent.
