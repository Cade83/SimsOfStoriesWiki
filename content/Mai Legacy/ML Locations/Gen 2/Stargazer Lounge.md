The **Stargazer Lounge** is a popular venue located on a skyscraper in [[San Myshuno]].

## Profile
The Stargazer Lounge is situated in an higher-end district of [[San Myshuno]], and therefore has a view of the entire city below. It's frequented by the wealthy and important, but its doors are open to all. As a result, it developed a lofty reputation, and its prices reflect said reputation. 

[[Jacques Villareal]] had his first kiss with [[Akira Kibo]] here, and later held their wedding at the lounge as well, determined to recreate their first kiss.

[[Judith-Noelle Gruber]] and [[Gretchen Zhou]] met at the Stargazer Lounge to discuss which of the two of them would ascend to the position of CEO of their company, as they were both being eyed for the role.

At [[Jocelyn Dishman]]'s request to have a rooftop wedding, [[Seth Mai]] held his to her wedding here, which was attended by many of San Myshuno's elite.
