**Jamie Mai** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Mai Legacy]].  She's the daughter of [[Daniela Mai|Daniela]] and [[Ed Mai|Ed]], born in [[James Mai|James]]'s absence.

## Profile
Jamie was born a few years after her older brother [[James Mai|James]] disappeared. Her parents, [[Daniela Mai|Daniela]] and [[Ed Mai|Ed]], had driven James away by continuously pressuring him. Jamie was made well aware of the fact that her brother was missing to such an extent that it searches for him and discussions about him lasted as some of her oldest memories. Even once her parents discovered where James was, everything, even down to her name being 'Jamie', made Jamie feel as if she was the replacement.

Unlike James, as Jamie grew up, her parents tried their best to leave her alone. They buried themselves in their work at the Mai Resort and Spa, trying their best to keep it afloat, even as their finances began to turn. At around this time, Jamie met [[Ty Cleary]] when he brought fresh produce from his farm to the kitchen at the resort. Their began meeting up every time he dropped by, and soon enough, a romance blossomed between them. They were married a couple years after Jamie graduated from high school, at which point she moved to [[Cleary Ranch]]. Daniela and Ed chose to close up shop and join her, seeing the writing on the wall about their resort.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.28.1 picture.png]] 
> A photograph of Jamie and her parents at Cleary Ranch.

[[Colette Cleary|Colette]] was born a year after Jamie moved in with Ty. Jamie and Ty put Colette to work at an early age, having her help out around the ranch with the smaller tasks. Colette's workload increased as Daniela and Ed's health declined, forcing Jamie to shift her focus. While her parents prepared for a fantastical visit from James, Jamie handled the sale of the Mai Resort and Spa's land and negotiated deals for Ed's paintings. Once Ed passed, Daniela begged Jamie to reach out to James on her behalf, but Jamie refused, feeling that James didn't deserve her company. Daniela passed without meeting James again as well.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.27.2.png]] 
> Jamie meets James for the first time.

When James finally did return home, he was an old man. Despite her dislike of the man, Jamie welcomed him in, showed him the letters her parents had saved, and told him the story of what had become of their parents, as it was what they would have wanted. At the same time, she was certain to let James know exactly what she thought of him for abandoning his family. Once she got it all out, however, she made a shaky peace with James for the sake of Daniela and Ed, and they exchanged contact info of their respective families.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[M.2.1.7.png]] 
> Jamie phones James's family.

During James's visit, he spoke with Colette. Colette, who had long idolized James, chose to emulate him by running away to [[Windenburg]]. The morning after she ran away and Jamie and Ty discovered that she was missing, Jamie immediately blamed James. Refusing to make the same mistakes her parents did, Jamie began phoning all of James's family, determined to get to the bottom of where Colette had gone.

## Personality
Jamie grew up with a deep seated bitterness towards [[James Mai|James]]. [[Daniela Mai|Daniela]] and [[Ed Mai|Ed]] seemed completely unable to move past him, even once they found out how he was doing, which was something that frustrated Jamie to no end, as she was always there in person, doing more to help out around the resort than James ever had. She felt under appreciated. These feelings endured into adulthood, which she unleashed on James personally. Regardless, Jamie still has a great deal of respect for her parents, and chose to honor their final wishes regarding James.

[[Ty Cleary|Ty]], on the other hand, is forever a bright light in Jamie's life. He appreciates her for her, and never makes her feel second best. While they might get into occasional arguments, Jamie is convinced that he is the best thing that's ever happened to her, and always chooses to let things go with him. With [[Colette Cleary|Colette]], Jamie fears repeating the mistakes of her parents and raised her with an iron grip. This led to regular conflicts between the two of them, but all that did was reaffirm to Jamie that they had a real relationship, where issues were aired out.
