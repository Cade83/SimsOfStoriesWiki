**Ulrike Faust** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Mai Legacy]]. 

## Profile
Ulrike dedicated much of her youth to the arts, and at university, she studied art history extensively. After years of hard work, she eventually worked her way up to the lofty title of curator of the famous [[Casbah Gallery]] in [[San Myshuno]]. Tasked with sourcing art for the world-famous art museum, Ulrike established an extensive web of connections within the art world. Her keen eye became highly regarded, as she made a habit of recognizing budding talent, nurturing it, and presenting it on the world stage, elevating many a career.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.8.16.png]] 
> The Renegades form.

Ulrike's work led to a meeting between herself and [[Jacques Villareal]], a well known art aficionado with connections of his own. The two of them bonded over their similar tastes and became good friends who regularly went and critiqued art together. This eventually became a business partnership, with Jacques sourcing valuable items Ulrike was interested in that she traded for paintings to Jacques's taste that the Casbah Gallery had no interest in. When Jacques's wife, [[Danna Villareal|Danna]], vanished, Ulrike followed his son, [[Max Villareal|Max]] on his request, as Max was lashing out. Citing struggles in the art world as a frustration for her, she ended up being pulled into a group of outcasts he formed, called the [[Renegades]].

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.8.5 v2.png]] 
> Akira introduces Ulrike to James.

Ulrike's friendship with Jacques led to a business partnership with [[Antonio Sanchez]]. It also later connected her with [[Akira Kibo]], Jacques's second spouse. Through Akira, Ulrike was a introduced to [[James Mai]], a budding artist that she quickly took interest in promoting, and she was soon immersed in James and Akira's friend group. This time in her life was short-lived, however, as Jacques suffered a heart failure. His passing shook Ulrike to her core, which wasn't aided by Akira keeping Jacques's health a secret from her.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.10.13 v2.png]] 
> Ulrike compliments artwork in James's home.

While Ulrike was part of James and Akira's friend group and attended their social gatherings, she felt distant from them, especially given long running issues James had with Jacques before his passing. Without Jacques, she felt rather lost, as he'd been her person. Antonio also cut off their business relationship during this time. With nowhere left to turn, Ulrike chose to actually commit to the Renegades, as they felt the most authentic out of all of her options. She became a motherly figure to each of the members.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.19.14.png]] 
> Ulrike meets Ivan.

One of Ulrike's greatest blessings ironically came from James's friend group. She chose to attend of of their gatherings for the first time in quite a while, and at that gathering, she encountered [[Ivan Dishman]]. The two of them instantly clicked over their shared criticism of Ivan's sister, [[Jocelyn Dishman|Jocelyn]]. Ulrike, who felt incredibly disconnected from one of her primary friend groups, and Ivan, who had long felt inferior to his sister, recognized each other as outcasts, fell hard for each other, then eloped. For the first time since Jacques's passing, Ulrike felt like she had her person again.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[K.1.7.4.png]] 
> Ulrike and Ivan with the Renegades.

Once the two of them married, Ulrike invited Ivan to join the Renegades with her. For several years, they all enjoyed contributing nothing to society whenever they met up in the [[Villareal Estate]]'s basement. Outside of Renegades meeting, Ulrike maintained the Casbah Gallery, but over the decades, the work had become increasingly monotonous to her. Therefore, when Antonio approached the Renegades with a very illegal work opportunity, despite Ulrike's apprehension to work with him again, she agreed merely to shake her life up.

## Personality
Ulrike is an incredibly emotionally distant woman and struggles to connect to others and the world at large. As the art curator for the [[Casbah Gallery]], she's radiant, and easily able to maintain surface-level, transactional relationships, but anything deeper is typically a struggle for her. [[James Mai|James]] welcomed Ulrike into his friend group with open arms, but she struggled to connect with them on a personal level and eventually distanced herself. The only two people who were able to truly light Ulrike's life up are [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]] and [[Ivan Dishman|Ivan]], two men who she realized she'd truly do anything for.

Despite her immense success in her professional life, Ulrike is ruled by a deep sense of boredom and dissatisfaction that is only resolved through thrill. The [[Renegades]] spoke to her as a group of 'outcasts' determined to make things worse for other people purely because they were nonconventional and similarly dissatisfied with the state of their lives. Ulrike's outlandish fashion sense and the very foundations of her relationship with Ivan are rooted in her desire to set herself apart from the crowd.
