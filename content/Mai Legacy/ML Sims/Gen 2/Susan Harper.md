**Susan Harper** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Mai Legacy]]. 

## Profile
Susan grew up in [[San Myshuno]]'s Fashion District where, at the insistence of her parents, she became a mentor to the younger kids in the neighborhood, such as [[Akira Kibo]]. By the time she was a teenager, Susan was over being pressured by her parents and chose to study abroad in [[Willow Creek]], where she became good friends with [[Eliza Pancakes]] and got to know her boyfriend at the time, [[Bob Pancakes|Bob]]. 

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.9.5.2.png]] 
> Susan celebrates Akira and Jacques's first kiss.

Once she graduated, Susan found herself back in the Fashion District where she rented an apartment of her own and began to compete in basketball tournaments and the womens soccer league for rent money, attracting the attention of [[Penny Pizzazz]], a rising star in the reporting world. While Susan and Penny became good friends, she chose not to spend much time with her parents from then on. Her bond with Akira, however, endured and they became more of friends than mentor and mentee once he turned 18. The two of them began to hit up bars, where Susan was introduced to Akira's friend [[Salim Benali]]. During this time, Susan also watched Akira meet [[Jacques Villareal]] and fall for him firsthand.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.2.4 v2.png]] 
> Susan meets James at karaoke night.

Akira was soon wed to Jacques, but martial troubles meant that he moved back to the Fashion District where he rented an apartment in the same building as Susan. Having the same experiences, the two of them protested the unfair rent imposed on them by their landlord and continued to go out to bars and party. One night, Akira was busy, so Susan ended up going to [[Planet Honeypop]] by herself where she competed against [[James Mai]] in a karaoke competition. James impressed her, and after a chat, the two exchanged contact info.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.3.5 v2.png]] 
> Susan spends a day with James.

Susan and James met up again a week later, and Susan introduced James to Akira, who was mid-protest. From there, they spent the day together, strolling around downtown [[Windenburg]] and hitting up a coffee shop, then eventually a pool. At the pool, James asked Susan out, which was something Susan found incredibly odd, as they'd spent all of two days together. She turned James down and left. As much as she'd enjoyed his company, he wanted things to move far too quickly.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.5.5 v2.png]] 
> Susan runs into James at Geekcon.

Unknown to Susan, Akira felt bad for James and kept talking to him. Wanting for the two of them to make amends, he invited them both to Geekcon, not letting either of them know he'd invited the other. James and Susan ran into each other, and at Akira's urging, they agreed to try at least being friends again. Their friendship was soon put to the test as Jacques, who was jealous of Akira's relationship with James, tried robbing [[Mai House|James's house]], and Susan and Akira had to put a stop to it themselves. That same day, Susan introduced James to Penny and Eliza, who was visiting. Eliza joked about Susan having feelings for James, making it very apparent that they existed.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.6.9.5.png]] 
> Susan and James get married.

Time passed, and Susan and James tried their best to ignore what Eliza had said, but Eliza, Akira, and Penny all kept pushing. They swore off anything romantic between each other, but chose to go to the Romance Festival, telling themselves it was to make fun of couples. As the evening progressed, Susan gave in and admitted to James that she liked him. In return, James proposed a crazy concept- a spontaneous wedding. Susan surprised herself by agreeing to it. They were able to shock their friends with the news when they met them at the [[Windenburg Ruins|ruins]] later that same night. Not long after the Romance Festival, Susan moved in with James. 

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.11.2 v2.png]] 
> Hajar confronts James and Susan.

Susan and James eventually gave birth to [[Seth Mai|Seth]], who was soon followed by [[Leah Mai|Leah]]. Life began to click into place for the two of them- Susan was bringing in money as well as a star basketball player, and James, who had wanted to establish his own legacy family, was beginning to attract attention with his artwork, making them a real contender. The house itself was coming together as well. All of this success attracted the attention of the Le Chien family, another legacy family. [[Hajar Cresques|Hajar]], the wife of [[Joaquin Le Chien]], current heir to the Le Chien legacy entered Susan and James's home and snapped a photo of their legacy armor. Susan and James chased her back to the [[Le Chien House]], where Joaquin's parents [[Rosaline Le Chien|Rosaline]] and [[Bronson Le Chien|Bronson]] served the Mai family with a lawsuit.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.14.6.png]] 
> Susan yells during their second day in court.

The Le Chien family required James and Susan to fly out to the [[Goth Brooks Courthouse]], where, despite their best efforts, [[Ethan Marshal|Judge Marshal]] ruled in favor of the Mais, stating that there was no precedent stating that two legacy families couldn't coexist. While Susan and James celebrated, and family friend [[Aarohi Barclay]] watched Seth and Leah, Hajar led Joaquin in a devious scheme to get revenge on the Mai family by aging up their newborn kids, saying that she was taking history for history. The following morning, the Le Chien's legacy armor was gone, and they all wound up back in court, where Judge Marshal again ruled in favor of the Mai family, as there was nothing implicating them in the armor's disappearance.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.17.9.png]] 
> Susan lectures Seth and Leah.

Finding themselves suddenly thrust into the role of parents of significantly older children, Susan was heartbroken. She loved both Seth and Leah dearly, but felt that they'd missed out on all the childhood memories and development they deserved. Having missed that critical stage, Susan and James chose to take a somewhat hands-off approach. They of course educated the two of them on what a legacy family was, their history with the Le Chiens, and what inheriting the legacy meant, but beyond that, they let the two of them develop their own opinions, but they weren’t afraid to reprimand them if it was needed, especially throughout their teenage relationship drama.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.20.2.png]] 
> Susan showing off her garden to her kids.

As their friends aged and changed, Susan and James got increasingly distant from their once strong friend group. They still talked with Akira and Salim on occasion, and Aarohi still helped with Seth and Leah, but it became increasingly challenging to plan things for everyone, especially once Seth and Leah married [[Jocelyn Dishman|Jocelyn]] and [[Maverick Holm|Maverick]]. Once Susan retired, she focused her efforts on tending to a garden she built up on the side of the house and assisting James secretly with the [[Mai Cemetery]].

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.26.10.png]] 
> Susan offers James encouragement before court.

As one final act of vengeance, Joaquin set Susan's garden ablaze at Hajar's urging. This time, James and Susan required the Le Chiens to fly down to the Goth Brooks Courthouse. Hajar was in a wheelchair, and when rising to her feet to make a point, she collapsed, and Joaquin wasn't willing to save her. This was a wakeup call for James, who went to visit his parents. Susan, meanwhile, returned home and helped prepare the Mai Cemetery, sensing that they didn't have much time left.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[M.1.31.17.png]] 
> Susan bids her last farewell to James.

Not long after James returned home, he ended up in the [[Windenburg Hospital|hospital]]. He passed away with his entire family in attendance, with Susan holding his hand. James had created his legacy. Once James passed, Susan got to work and planned his funeral and the ascension of his heir. A short time later, at James's funeral Susan passed on the legacy to Leah. It was a decision James, Susan, and their close friends had made collectively at one final gathering before he passed. 

## Personality
Susan is an incredibly caring and friendly person, going all the way back to her childhood acting as a mentor to [[Akira Kibo|Akira]]. She chooses to see the best in people, even those like [[Jacques Villareal|Jacques]], who are unquestionably cruel to everyone. Once that line is finally crossed, however, Susan is done with that person for good and is willing to do anything to bring them to their knees.

[[James Mai|James]] basically crossed that line when he asked Susan out on their second day together. Susan sound him to be creepy, and if it hadn't been for Akira's urging, she doubts she would've ever spoken to him again. She couldn't be more grateful to Akira, as her 'karaoke boy' became the most important person in her life. Susan would do anything for James. She loves deeply, both romantically, parentally, and with friends such as [[Penny Pizzazz|Penny]] and [[Eliza Pancakes|Eliza]].

Susan enjoys poking a bit of fun at others, especially those she loves, making small quips in an endearing way. Many of her words are spoken with intention and boldness, and she can command a room when she wants to, although that's something Susan rarely ever wants to do. In her old age, she found herself having to do more of that, and it began to wear on her.
