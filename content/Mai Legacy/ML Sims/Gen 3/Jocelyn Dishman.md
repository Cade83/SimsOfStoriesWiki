**Jocelyn Dishman** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Mai Legacy]].

## Profile
Jocelyn never met her birth parents. She was put up for adoption shortly after being born and stayed at an orphanage for five years until [[Sara Dishman|Sara]] and [[Rene Dishman]] chose to adopt her and her future adoptive brother, [[Ivan Dishman|Ivan]]. Sara, who'd been very successful professionally, chose to sell her company and retire so she could focus on being a full-time mom, and Rene joined her. Jocelyn and Ivan did not get along and in dealing with the two of them Sara, unfortunately, discovered that was ill-suited to being a parent. With Rene giving questionable guidance, as well, Jocelyn grew up largely left to her own devices. She spent a lot of time in her room reading tabloids, and in this time, she came to truly admire and aspire to be like [[Lily Feng]], who to her represented what success looked like.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.16.9.png]] 
> Jocelyn and Seth hit it off.

After graduating from school, Jocelyn took on a serving job at [[Le Café de la Pain]]. While working one day, she spotted [[Seth Mai]] stalking his sister, [[Leah Mai|Leah]] and her date, [[Maverick Holm]]. All three of them had attended the same school as hers, and she was familiar with the Mai family from her tabloids, so Jocelyn chose to confront Seth for his questionable behavior. Seth explained himself, and once Jocelyn understood his situation, the two of them hit it off, even exchanging contact info.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.19.13.png]] 
> Jocelyn descends the stairs to greet Seth.

Seth invited Jocelyn over to [[Mai House|his house]], and Jocelyn got to meet his parents [[James Mai|James]] and [[Susan Harper|Susan]], which left her feeling somewhat starstruck. She'd seen the two of them in her tabloids since she was young. Jocelyn and Seth clicked well enough that Sara and Rene were came to believe that the two of them were secretly dating. They asked Jocelyn to invite Seth's family over to [[Dishman House|their house]], where Seth surprised everyone, even Jocelyn, by announcing that he and Jocelyn were already dating. Jocelyn genuinely liked Seth, but she was convinced that his sudden announcement was due to him desperately needing a beard to hide his sexuality, which she chose to go along with.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.20.19.png]] 
> Jocelyn and Seth get married.

After a few months of dating, Seth invited Jocelyn out for a dinner at a [[Summer's Restaurant|fancy restaurant]] where he told Jocelyn that he'd learned that he was genuinely attracted to her and wanted to spend the rest of his life with her. Jocelyn happily accepted his proposal. Jocelyn's relationship with Seth further worsened her relationship with Ivan, with him beginning to outwardly express his jealousy. While Sara and Rene dictated many parts of their wedding, Jocelyn got her wish to get married somewhere with a few of the city with the venue being the [[Stargazer Lounge]]. 

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.23.10.png]] 
> Jocelyn sobs over her parents at Leah's wedding.

Not long after the wedding, Jocelyn got pregnant with [[Ronnie]], but she wasn't yet ready to move in with Seth. She continued living with her family until their house burnt to the ground, taking the lives of Sara and Rene. After that day, Jocelyn moved in with Seth, as most reminders of her parents were painful. Leah's wedding to Maverick was particular painful for her, as it reminded her of how involved her parents had been in hers and due to Ivan continuing to be cruel to her, consumed by his jealousy. Ronnie was born later that same night.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[M.1.30.17.png]] 
> Maverick speaks with Jocelyn after Seth's surgery.

Once Maverick moved into the Mai House, Jocelyn started to truly connect with him.They were able to relate to each other as two ordinary people who married into a legacy time, two people who had both been the cause of arguments between Leah and Seth, and two who had lost their parents young. Both unemployed, they spent a lot of time looking after Ronnie and Leah and Maverick's daughter, [[Willow Holm|Willow]] together. When Seth got dramatic plastic surgery to improve his appearance despite Jocelyn's reassurances that she loved him for who he was, Maverick was the only person able to comfort her.

## Personality
Jocelyn has a very sharp sense of wit, often saying things at her own expense or others close to her, making her very enjoyable to talk to. Her time dating [[Seth Mai|Seth]] when she believed he was closeted are emblematic of this, as she was convinced she had no chance with him, but she stuck by his side while making fun of herself for reaping what benefits she could while dating who she believed was a gay man. 

Jocelyn has great admiration for the wealthy and powerful, aspiring to live up to the image of [[Lily Feng|Lily]], [[James Mai|James]], and [[Susan Harper|Susan]] as portrayed in the tabloids she used to live, so the opportunity to become close to them was thrilling to her. Jocelyn's relationship with Seth gradually evolved beyond that point, and finding out that he actually loved her romantically was one of the greatest days in her life. He'd already become family to her at that point, and Jocelyn greatly values family, even if her original family became rather dysfunctional due to [[Sara Dishman|Sara]]'s distance and [[Ivan Dishman|Ivan]]'s jealousy. She felt the loss of her parents keenly and was grateful that [[Maverick Holm|Maverick]] was around to understand her pain.
