**Maverick Holm** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Mai Legacy]]. 

## Profile
Maverick grew up without his parents in his life, raised solely by his aunt in her somewhat rundown [[Maverick’s Apartment|apartment]] in the Spice District of [[San Myshuno]]. His aunt didn't talk much about his parents, both of whom had died in a car accident when he was 10, so Maverick came to accept that his family history was something he'd never fully know or understand. His aunt was all he needed. She worked hard as a mechanic to provide for him, and even managed to save up enough to get him into a good school.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.15.3.png]] 
> Maverick gets to know Leah.

The school he attended was also attended by [[Seth Mai|Seth]] and [[Leah Mai]], the children of an up and coming legacy family that had captivated the media's attention in that time due to their feud with the Le Chien family. Maverick ended up in one of the same classes as Leah, and she took an interest in him, even inviting him over to her [[Mai House|home]]. At Leah's place, Maverick came to understand that Leah was convinced she could have any guy in the world, and that she was in competition with Seth to have a significant other first, made clear when Seth made a move on him.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.16.4.png]] 
> Leah treats Maverick to lunch to discuss Seth.

Maverick found himself very confused by the Mai family after his first time over. Leah seemed rude and above him, and Seth made him uncertain about his feelings. Leah took him out to a [[Le Café de la Pain|cafe]] to discuss things and tried her best to smooth things over. Maverick was intrigued enough to keep hanging around, and he gradually got to know her parents, [[James Mai|James]] and [[Susan Harper|Susan]]. The two of them eventually made it exclusive, but Leah insisted that they had to keep it quiet because her parents weren't fond of the the fighting he created between her and Seth.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.23.1.png]] 
> Maverick shows up on Leah's doorstep.

After Maverick and Leah made things secretly exclusive, Leah suddenly got distant. One day, Leah phoned him and asked to meet in a [[Hare Square|park]], where she told him she suffered from crippling commitment issues. While Maverick insisted it was okay, Leah felt that it wasn't fair to Maverick and cut him off. She ignored all his attempts to reach her until he showed up on her doorstep 8 months later. Upon seeing him again, Leah gave in, because she'd missed him and couldn't picture herself with anyone else. She promised Maverick she'd work on her issues for him.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.23.13.png]] 
> Maverick and Leah at their wedding.

With everything right between the two of them again, Maverick proposed, then the wedding followed shortly after. It was a smaller affair set up in a [[Forest Clearing|clearing]] in the forest near the Mai House, decorated with an abundance of lights and greenery. The wedding was attended by all of James and Susan's friends. Shortly after, Maverick and Leah gave birth to [[Willow Holm|Willow]], and Maverick moved in with Leah's family to be with his wife, get to better know his in-laws, and take care of his new daughter.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[M.1.31.10.png]] 
> Maverick and Jocelyn chat about their shared trauma.

Once moved in with the Mai family, Maverick got close with Seth's wife, [[Jocelyn Dishman]]. They were able to relate to each other as two ordinary people who married into a legacy time, two people who had both been the cause of arguments between Leah and Seth, and two who had lost their parents young. Both unemployed, they spent a lot of time looking after Willow and Seth and Jocelyn's son, [[Ronnie Mai|Ronnie]], together. Maverick and Jocelyn gradually became each other's rocks during times of strife.

## Personality
Maverick tends to go with the flow with most things in his life. When his aunt chose not to speak about his parents, he accepted it, when [[Leah Mai|Leah]] expressed interest in him, he rolled with it, and he even questioned himself when [[Seth Mai|Seth]] kissed him. Maverick doesn't believe in fate, firmly believing that everyone makes their own choices and he chooses to be easy-going and cause as few problems for others as possible. That isn't to say that he'll ignore a potential problem once it arises, however. 

Leah's issues with commitment were an issue Maverick couldn't sweep under the rug. In his time with her, he'd come to love Leah and her family, flaws and all, and he was more than willing to work with her to overcome what she was dealing with. The Mai family does cause Maverick no end of headaches, but he deals with it for Leah and [[Willow Holm|Willow]] and is grateful that [[Jocelyn Dishman|Jocelyn]] is there to understand and relate to his frustrations.
