**Colette Cleary** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Mai Legacy]].  She's the daughter of [[Jamie Mai]] and [[Ty Cleary]].

## Profile
Colette was born to [[Jamie Mai|Jamie]] and [[Ty Cleary|Ty]] and even from a young age, she could feel her mother's frustrations. He grandparents, [[Daniela Mai|Daniela]] and [[Ed Mai|Ed]] would tell her stories about their missing son, [[James Mai|James]], and as Ty and Jamie put her to work on the [[Cleary Ranch|family ranch]], she dreamed of running away like he had, and never having to see her mother again. Even after her grandparents passed, James became a borderline mythical figure for Colette, a man who had ran away and achieved so much, a man with a story that she felt was unachievable for her. She was stuck working in the heat and the dust, doing menial tasks like milking cows with Jamie yelling at her whenever Colette got frustrated and screwed up, while James had achieved success and fame.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.27.17.png]] 
> Colette begs James to take her with him.

When James actually came to visit his family, Colette felt as if her dreams were coming true. Her opportunity to flee her life had finally come knocking. She pulled James aside, and begged him to take her to [[Windenburg]] with him, even if it pissed off Jamie and Ty. James refused, telling her that he wasn't willing to separate her from her home, but he did make sure she got his contact info by giving it to Jamie.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[M.1.30.20.png]] 
> Colette waits for the bus.

Once James left, Colette angrily returned to her typical work on the ranch. Nothing ever changed for her, she felt trapped. In her rage, she slammed a door, which made Jamie yell at her and Ty have to reprimand her about slamming doors. Beyond fed up, Colette went to retrieve her phone and found a text from [[Leah Mai]], James's daughter, telling Colette that she had had support if she ran away. Colette started plotting her escape plan that very night, and soon enough, she was [[Oasis Springs Bustop|waiting to catch a bus]] that would kick off her journey to Windenburg.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[M.2.1.15.png]] 
> Jocelyn takes Colette shopping.

When Colette finally got to the [[Mai House]] in Windenburg, James had recently passed away. Too concerned with everything else going on to deal with Colette immediately, [[Susan Harper]], James's wife, took her in without argument. [[Jocelyn Dishman]], the wife of James's son [[Seth Mai|Seth]], was tasked with taking Colette shopping for clothes, and while out shopping with her, Colette felt loved for the first time.

## Personality
While living at the [[Cleary Ranch]], Colette felt a great deal of frustration with her situation. Her relationship with [[Jamie Mai|Jamie]] was tenuous at best, [[Ty Cleary|Ty]] required too much of her, and she hated the weather and her job. She often felt as if she wasn't loved, and was appreciated for her work instead of her own self. [[James Mai|James]] became an idealized figure for Colette, an example of opportunity if she took the risk and ran, like he had.
