**Leah Mai** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Mai Legacy]]. She's the daughter of [[James Mai|James]], and the sister of [[Seth Mai|Seth]]. She inherits the Mai Legacy after her father's passing.

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.14.14.png]] 
> Leah dining with her family in her youth.

Leah and her older brother [[Seth Mai|Seth]] were born and raised in the public eye, growing up with the knowledge that one day, only one of them could succeed their father, [[James Mai|James]], and inherit the Mai Legacy. Leah tragically missed out on a significant part of her childhood, as during her parents' feud with the Le Chien family, [[Hajar Cresques]] broke into their [[Mai House|home]] and aged up Leah and Seth with a potion, robbing them of their early developmental years. James and their mother, [[Susan Harper|Susan]], educated Leah on her role, but left figuring out how to earn the title of heir to her.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.15.4.png]] 
> Leah tells Maverick she's interested in him.

James and Susan's hand off approach to parenting led to Leah and Seth developing a bitter rivalry as they grew up, both having been taught repeatedly  that only one of them could inherit the legacy. Leah rushed into things and brought [[Maverick Holm]], her classmate, home to tell him she was interested in him and that he'd better take her up on her offer. When she left the room, Seth chose to approach him romantically as well, making Leah furious for both not respecting her and forcing her to have an awkward conversation with Maverick. Luckily for Leah, Maverick chose to stick around.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.17.7.png]] 
> Leah exposes Seth to her parents.

Leah kept bringing Maverick over, where he was interrogated by Susan and close family friend [[Aarohi Barclay]]. While he was over, [[Jocelyn Dishman]], a girl Seth had met, dropped by, and in their efforts to one up each other, they exposed all the underhanded tactics they'd used to try to get a spouse before their competition. James and Susan laid into both Leah and Seth for this, and from that day on, they chose to keep anything underhanded quiet. Having everything out in the open must have encouraged Maverick, as he asked Leah to make things official shortly after. She kept this quiet outside of gloating to Seth, as neither James or Susan were fond of Maverick because of the drama he brought into the house.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.21.10.png]] 
> Leah runs away from Maverick.

Time passed, and Seth got married to Jocelyn, which made Leah start to realize just how distant she'd kept Maverick. They'd been dating for ages, but she'd been terrified to take the next step, which Leah came to realize was commitment issues. Leah called up Maverick and told him, apologizing profusely. Maverick tried his best to reassure her, but Leah fled and cut him off. She stopped responding to his texts as well. She threw herself into looking into what it'd take to be a talk show host instead.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.23.17.png]] 
> Leah and Maverick get married.

Eight months later, Maverick showed up on Leah's doorstep, and she came to realize that she missed him. Telling Maverick that she'd learn to deal with her commitment issues, as she truly couldn't see herself with anyone else. This dramatically accelerated their relationship, and within a month, the two were wed in a charming affair held in [[Forest Clearing|a clearing]] in the forest near the Mai House. It was attended by all of James and Susan's friends. Not long after, Leah gave birth to [[Willow Holm|Willow]].

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.26.8.png]] 
> Leah interviews Seth.

Leah had continued to work on setting up her talk show, which she called *[[The Afternoon Tea]]*. The night before her debut episode was set to record, [[Joaquin Le Chien]] set fire to a garden Susan had made for Leah and Seth years ago, giving Leah her first discussion topic. Seth attending the filming to support Leah. Her director, [[Karen Galindo]], suggested that Leah have Seth on as a guest, but as Seth wasn't prepared, she quickly pivoted to taking questions. None of the questions were prescreened, Seth received several questions attacking his appearance, and he fled the studio.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.28.10.png]] 
> Leah texts Colette her advice.

When James came back from a visit home and told his family about their extended family, Leah was intrigued by her cousin, [[Colette Cleary]], who wanted to run away from her country life. Leah chose to borrow James's phone and text Colette, telling her to go for it and that she'd support her if she made it to [[Windenburg]]. This soon became something Leah forgot about, as Seth secretly went through intensive plastic surgery to improve his appearance, followed by James collapsing and being rushed to the [[Windenburg Hospital|hospital]]. Colette came to Windenburg that same day.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[M.2.1.36.png]] 
> Leah asks Susan questions about legacy families.

James passed away in the hospital, and his funeral was held at the recently constructed [[Mai Cemetery]], which had been one of James's final projects. The funeral was hounded by paparazzi, eager to see who would inherit the legacy. James, Susan, and each of their friends had all voted on who they wanted to see succeed James, and Leah was declared the new heir. Even Seth congratulated her. Susan then took her below-ground, where she told her all about how legacies functioned for the first time.

## Personality
Leah was raised knowing she might one day inherit the Mai Legacy, and she carried herself as if she'd become the future heir from a young age. Leah quite simply knew that the title was hers, and that knowledge allowed her to look down on others and parade around as if the world was owed to her. This intense sense of over-confidence leaked over to other aspects of her life, such as her early belief that she'd be able to pick from any man in the world for a spouse, or her fully believing she could masterfully run *[[The Afternoon Tea]]* before the first episode ever filmed.

Leah's relationship with [[Seth Mai|Seth]] is tenuous at best, with the two of them having been raised by [[James Mai|James]] and [[Susan Harper|Susan]] to be in constant competition with each other. In her younger years, she bullied Seth relentlessly over his inability to find a partner as quickly and for lacking the confidence she radiated. Leah struggled to understand how her words might have potentially affected Seth, which is a downfall of hers, as she repeated a similar mistake with [[Colette Cleary|Colette]] by encouraging her to make such a drastic decision.

One of Leah's biggest struggles is in her relationship with love and commitment. She connected with [[Maverick Holm|Maverick]] early on in her life, and while she genuinely adored him as a person, she struggled making any commitment to him, even keeping the fact that they'd made themselves exclusive to herself at first. This came to bother her immensely, and while she tried cutting Maverick off, it only made her realize that she couldn't live life without him.

Leah has a great deal of respect for her family and the values instilled in her by her parents. While she was cruel to Seth in her youth, she respected his decisions as a result.
