**Seth Mai** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Mai Legacy]]. He's the son of [[James Mai|James]], and the brother of [[Leah Mai|Leah]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.15.1.png]] 
> Seth with his family in his youth.

Seth and his younger sister [[Leah Mai|Leah]] were born and raised in the public eye, growing up with the knowledge that one day, only one of them could succeed their father, [[James Mai|James]], and inherit the Mai Legacy. Seth tragically missed out on a significant part of his childhood, as during his parents' feud with the Le Chien family, [[Hajar Cresques]] broke into their [[Mai House|home]] and aged up Seth and Leah with a potion, robbing them of their early developmental years. James and their mother, [[Susan Harper|Susan]], educated Seth on his role, but left figuring out how to earn the title of heir to him.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.15.8.png]] 
> Seth makes a move on Maverick.

James and Susan's hand off approach to parenting led to Seth and Leah developing a bitter rivalry as they grew up, both having been taught repeatedly that only one of them could inherit the legacy. Knowing he was in direct competition with Leah and that finding a spouse first could make or break him, Seth tried to steal [[Maverick Holm]] from Leah when she brought him home from school. When this didn't work, he even followed them to a [[La Café de la Pain|cafe]], hoping to ruin things for the two of them. 

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.16.7.png]] 
> Seth meets Jocelyn.

At that cafe, Seth was confronted by [[Jocelyn Dishman]], an employee at the cafe who thought he was stalking Leah, and after he explained himself, they hit it off. He later invited Jocelyn over, where she got to meet James and Susan. Unfortunately for Seth, Maverick was also there with Leah. Seth accidentally exposed the fact that he'd followed Leah to the cafe, which led to Leah exposing what Seth had done to Maverick, and gradually, all the underhanded tactics they'd used to try to get a spouse before their competition were out in the open. James and Susan laid into both Seth and Leah for this, and from that day on, they chose to keep anything underhanded quiet.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.19.17.png]] 
> Seth tells Sara and Rene that he and Jocelyn are dating.

With what he'd done with Maverick out in the open and Jocelyn now in the picture, Seth decided to figure out his sexuality. He approached [[Akira Kibo]] and [[Salim Benali]], two close family friends who had recently gotten married, and they tried their best to advise him. When Leah made things official with Maverick, Seth was shaken to his core. While attending a party at [[Dishman House|Jocelyn's house]], Seth made a split second decision and lied to her parents, [[Sara Dishman|Sara]] and [[Rene Dishman|Rene]], saying that he and Jocelyn were dating as well. Jocelyn, luckily, had no issue with it.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[1.20.13.png]] 
> Seth and Jocelyn take a selfie for an article for Penny.

Soon enough, Seth has proposed to Jocelyn, and they were planning their wedding. Sara and Rene dictated the dress code, but all Jocelyn asked for was a wedding with a view of the city, so Seth rented out the [[Stargazer Lounge]]. Their wedding was attended by all of their friends and family, and even Leah congratulated Seth. At the wedding, Seth spoke to [[Penny Pizzazz]], famed journalist, for advice about entering journalism, and she recommended he speak to Salim. Not long after the wedding, Jocelyn got pregnant with [[Ronnie Mai|Ronnie]].

> [!caption|left]
> ![[1.26.3.png]] 
> Seth and Leah cross the harbor to get to Leah's studio.

Leah had worked on setting up for her own talk show, *[[The Afternoon Tea]]*, and for her debut episode, Seth attended the filming to support her. [[Joaquin Le Chien]] had torched a garden Susan had created for the two of them the night before filming, giving Leah something to discuss. At the suggestion of Leah's director, [[Karen Galindo]], Seth was brought on as a guest, but when they started taking questions, some of them directly attacking Seth's appearance, leading to him fleeing the studio.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[M.1.30.8.png]] 
> Seth returns home with his new face.

Those comments wounded Seth deeply, making him doubt even his marriage to Jocelyn and ability to inherit the legacy. He chose to meet with [[Miguel Brock|Doctor Brock]] to pursue plastic surgery to make his face look more conventionally attractive. He went through with the operation without informing anyone, and horrified Jocelyn when he came back, as he looked like a totally different person. Leah, ironically enough, apologized to Seth and offered to prepare the rest of the family for his sudden change in appearance. 

> [!caption|left]
> ![[M.2.1.31.png]] 
> Seth congratulates Leah on inheriting the legacy.

That night, James collapsed, was rushed to the [[Windenburg Hospital|hospital]], and ultimately passed away. His funeral was held in the [[Mai Cemetery]], which had been one of James's final projects. At the funeral, which was hounded by paparazzi, Susan announced that James, herself, and each of their friends had voted on who they wanted to see succeed James. The title of heir was given to Leah, and Seth accepted his defeat with grace. He'd seen it coming beforehand, as even he believed that Leah had accomplished more than him.

## Personality
Seth was raised by [[James Mai|James]] and [[Susan Harper|Susan]] with the knowledge that he might one day inherit the Mai Legacy, but unlike [[Leah Mai|Leah]], all this did for Seth was fill him with doubt. He developed insecurity in his capabilities, and constantly comparing himself to Leah, who made things happen with her intense self-confidence, only made things worse. This only got worse when later in life, he began to hear criticism about his appearance from the public, driving him to seek cosmetic surgery.

Seth spends a lot of time in his own head, mulling over every thought and feeling. This kind of thinking only helps him spiral when he enters a dark headspace, but it's also helped him realize things about himself much quicker than Leah ever could. He has a great degree of emotional intelligence as a result. When he's in a bad place, [[Jocelyn Dishman|Jocelyn]] is one of the only people who can pull him out of it. He'd be lost without her.

When Seth doesn't have the time to think things over, he has a tendency to make quick, poorly thought out decisions out of panic. One of his earliest was kissing [[Maverick Holm|Maverick]], but this continued on to his sudden exclusivity with Jocelyn and storming off set when receiving questions about his appearance on *[[The Afternoon Tea]]*, which only made things worse for him publicly.

Despite all of their conflict over the legacy, when Leah won the title of heir, Seth accepted his defeat with grace. He honestly found it to be a relief by that point, and felt that Leah deserved it more.
