**The Renegades** are a group of delinquents led by [[Max Villareal]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[K.1.8.16.png]] 
> The Renegades form.

After his mother [[Danna Villareal|Danna]] vanished under mysterious circumstances, [[Max Villareal]] was determined to inflict his rage upon the world. While at [[Bathe de Rill]] one day, he began to graffiti the bathroom. He was joined by [[Ulrike Faust]], who was experiencing frustrations in the art business. They fled the bathroom together and ran into [[Morgan Fyres]] and [[Wolfgang Munch]], who were bickering with the staff for no reason. Max and Ulrike joined them, and once they all chatted later that evening, they formed the Renegades, complete with a chant the young Max came up with.

As the Renegades became increasingly unwelcome in public spaces, Max transformed the basement of the [[Villareal Estate]] into a hangout. From that day on, the four of them met in that basement regularly, with Max funding almost every expense with his own massive inheritance. Unfortunately, Wolfgang's membership was short-lived, as once he saw that his life was headed in a different direction where he was no longer an outcast, he bid his farewells and left, disgusting Max. After Wolfgang left, [[Ivan Dishman]] joined them when Ulrike invited him to the group, and he and Ulrike later encouraged [[Ronnie Mai]] to join as well.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[K.1.7.11.png]] 
> Antonio meets with the Renegades.

When [[Kiara Sanchez]] left home, her father, [[Antonio Sanchez|Antonio]] contacted the Renegades with a job opportunity that would become many if their first job went well. He wanted them to ensure that [[Josh Marks]] agreed to meet with him and his wife [[Jacqueline Sanchez|Jacqueline]] through any means necessary. Max secured incredible pay for each of them, then they got to work.

## Known Members
- [[Max Villareal]] - The leader and founder of the Renegades. He hosts their meetings and funds their activities.
- [[Ulrike Faust]] - An art dealer, she is the first person Max connected with. 
- [[Morgan Fyres]] - An ambitious girl who has aims on Max.
- [[Wolfgang Munch]] - The middle of three children, he joined because he hated his life, but as things changed for him, he made his departure.
- [[Ivan Dishman]] - Invited to the meetings by Ulrike, he joined due to his dislike of his [[Jocelyn Dishman|sister]].
- [[Ronnie Mai]] - The son of the powerful Mai legacy family, he joined because he felt his family's inheritance rules were unfair to him.
### Affiliates
- [[Antonio Sanchez]] - Cuts a deal with the Renegades where they get paid for various unpleasant tasks he has for them.
