The **Wedding Park** is a location that appears in [[Dreams of Fame]].

## Profile
Before becoming a park for weddings, the park was somewhat rundown and overgrown, located adjacent to the [[Presley House]]. The government had even repossessed it due to the state of disrepair it had fallen into. When [[Zoe Presley]] was told that [[Travis Scott]] and [[Liberty Lee]] needed a venue for their wedding, she purchased the plot of land from the government and hired people to renovate it and add a bridal suite and a groomsman suite, both of which were were built underground. Above ground, the entrance to the underground area became an open bar. Purchasing this plot of land also allowed Zoe to gain some much needed privacy, as her celebrity status meant people crept into the park to try and catch a glimpse of her or [[Giovanna Cardwell]].

Travis and Liberty were happily married in the park a short while after the renovations were completed.
