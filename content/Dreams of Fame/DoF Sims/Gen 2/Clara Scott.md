**Clara Scott** is one of the protagonists of the second chapter of [[Dreams of Fame]]. She's the daughter of [[Travis Scott]] and [[Liberty Lee]], and the younger sister of [[Jacob Scott]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[S.1.29.8.png]] 
> Clara and Jacob celebrate their birthday without Liberty.

In Clara's earliest years, she was raised with plenty of attention in the [[Presley House]], doted on by both [[Travis Scott]] and [[Liberty Lee]], as well as her grandmother, [[Giovanna Cardwell]]. She eclipsed her older brother [[Jacob Scott|Jacob]] almost immediately, but he never seemed too bothered by it. Things began to shift for Clara when Liberty left for [[Sixam]], leaving her with just Travis and Giovanna, as the other people living in the house at that point had little interest in parenting. They eventually ended up celebrating a birthday without Liberty, which became a core memory for both Clara and Jacob.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[S.1.31.7.png]] 
> Summer has Clara and Jacob make drinks.

Clara found it intriguing that Liberty was exploring space, but eventually, she came to care little for Liberty at all. She barely knew the woman, after all. Giovanna tried to connect with Clara with her stories, and while Clara found them entertaining, the clear fictionality of each tale made her raise an eyebrow. She found [[Jo Kauffman]] similar- a domineering old woman who required too much attention. The only person Clara truly connected with outside of Travis was [[Summer Holiday]], her aunt, who stepped in while Liberty was absent. She treated Clara like an adult, and taught her how to mix drinks at an early age, which was something Clara appreciated greatly.

## Personality
Clara is harsh with her words, and vocally critical of those around her without remorse.
