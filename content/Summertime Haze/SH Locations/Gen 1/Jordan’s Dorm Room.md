**Jordan & Hope’s Dorm Room** is a location in [[Summertime Haze]]. 

## Profile
[[Jordan Crespo]] and [[Hope Peck]] ended up assigned to the same doom room when they attended culinary school, which kicked off their friendship, their romance, and their rivalry. They were only in the room their freshman year, but it spurred on a relationship that would last the two of them a lifetime.
