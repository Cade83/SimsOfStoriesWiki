**Hope Peck** is a character in the first chapter of [[Summertime Haze]]. She's a friend and rival of [[Jordan Crespo]].

## Profile
> [!caption|right]
> ![[C.1.11.3.png]] 
> Hope argues with Jordan in their dorm.

Hope knew from a young age that she was going to grow up to be a chef. She went to catering school straight out of high school, where she was assigned [[Jordan Crespo]] as a roommate in the [[Jordan’s Dorm Room|dorms]]. The two of them developed a friendship early on, but once classes started, they became fierce rivals. Things became so ugly between the two of them that it became something they were both asked about years later in the professional careers. Despite their rivalry during this time, Hope and Jordan had a brief romantic fling, but neither of them enjoyed it.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[C.1.11.4.png]] 
> Hope offers Jordan a job opportunity.

In school, Hope focused on building professional relationships, while Jordan focused on grades. In the long term, this brought about greater success for Hope than Jordan. The two of them continued to live together and managed to set their rivalry aside with Hope making most of the money through an illustrious career coming up with new menu items for high end restaurants, even being flown out overseas on occasion. Feeling pity for Jordan, Hope frequently invited Jordan alongside her as her sous chef, hoping it would bring about opportunities for her.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[C.1.13.15.png]] 
> Hope entertains at one of Henry's get togethers.

At the suggestion of Jordan's friend, [[Bennett Reardon]], Hope was invited to a get together hosted by [[Henry Doyle]], where Bennett encouraged her to take on Jordan as a full time employee. At that get together, she got to know [[Toby Baxter]] and his friend, [[Paolo Rocca]], taking a special interest in his background and quiet, mysterious vibe. She also learned of Henry's intentions to form a support group opposed to his antagonizer [[Ellen Bowers]], which Hope readily agreed to support. She later befriended Toby's wife, [[Hayley Baxter]], as well, with the two of them bonding over cooking.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[C.1.17.22.png]] 
> Hope gives Jordan and Bennett a side-eye. She knows.

As she got to know everyone better, Hope quickly took note of Jordan's obvious attraction to Bennett. Hope connected with Henry and confirmed that Bennett liked Jordan too, then the two of them dedicated themselves to trying to do anything they could to get Jordan and Bennett to admit their feelings to each other. Hope even resorted to directly begging them to finally go out on a date. When Jordan and Bennett started dating in secret and only told Hope and Henry after the fact, they were astounded.

## Personality
Hope is fiercely protective of the people, things, and ideas she values. Her initial bitter rivalry with [[Jordan Crespo|Jordan]] stemmed from Jordan insulting her cooking, which Hope took too much pride in to tolerate. This protective nature later extended to Jordan herself, with Hope doing anything she can to try to keep her employed, and later [[Henry Doyle|Henry]], where she took [[Toby Baxter|Toby]]'s exit from his life and later disinvitation from [[Hayley Baxter|Hayley]]'s funeral as a slight against him and his entire friend group.

Outside of her intense defensiveness, Hope approaches life with a great deal of humor. She loves to have banter with Jordan and often makes offhand jokes at the expense of herself or others, such as claiming that she prefers 'bad boys' in response to learning about [[Paolo Rocca|Paolo]]'s past or her numerous jabs at her friends in her attempts to get Jordan and [[Bennett Reardon|Bennett]] to admit their feelings to each other.
