**Alina Reardon** is a character in the first chapter of [[Summertime Haze]]. She's the mother of [[Bennett Reardon|Bennett]] and [[Regan Reardon]].

## Profile
Alina grew up wanting to help others, and ended up going into social work after high school. This career path took her to the [[Willow Creek Police Station|police department]], where a social worker was needed to provide crisis response. She was assigned [[Layne Reardon]] as her partner, and the two of them went on to investigate many deeply personal crimes, gaining a key understanding of the human psyche. As they identified culprit after culprit, the pair of them began to build up a bit of a reputation- if a criminal committed a crime and got Alina and Layne involved, they were screwed.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[C.1.4.12.png]] 
> Alina greets Henry, one of Bennett's friends.

As partners, Alina gradually fell for Layne, and the two eventually got married, bought a [[Reardon House|house]], and had [[Bennett Reardon|Bennett]] and [[Regan Reardon|Regan]]. They were sure to give their children anything they wanted as they grew up, utilizing their rather large salaries. This support continued into their adulthood after their retirement from the force, with Alina and Layne supporting Bennett as a struggling artist post-grad and Alina trying her best to connect with Regan in her rebellious phase by giving her a greenhouse to manage and trying to get her enrolled in college.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[C.1.15.18.png]] 
> Alina informs Bennett and his friends about Regan.

When Regan ran away from home, Alina and Layne called up several of their cop friends and started putting their old investigation techniques to work. They spoke with Bennett and his friends [[Jordan Crespo]] and [[Henry Doyle]], attempting to locate the last person Regan had spoken to, but Regan had done too good of a job covering her tracks, and the trail had gone cold.

## Personality
Alina is a woman who cares deeply about other people and the world at large, which directed the entire course of her professional life. She became so good at what she did in the police force because she understood how other's minds worked- Alina, however, fell short in that regard when it came to [[Bennett Reardon|Bennett]] and [[Regan Reardon|Regan]]. She struggled removing herself from the equation and could only continue pushing her viewpoints and ideas.

Outside of work, Alina is fantastic at keeping up rapport with others. She loves to banter with [[Layne Reardon|Layne]], and will jump to offer her assistance to anyone who might possibly need it. When the situation calls for it, however, Alina can bury her own emotions to get very serious and command a room.
