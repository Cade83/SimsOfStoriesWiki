**Layne Reardon** is a character in the first chapter of [[Summertime Haze]]. He's the father of [[Bennett Reardon|Bennett]] and [[Regan Reardon]].

## Profile
Layne didn't do great in school and had no hobbies outside of a vague interest in cooking, so he chose to turn to [[Willow Creek Police Station|law enforcement]], unsure of what else he could do with his life. While working, he found that he had a knack for solving crimes, and gradually made his way up to investigation work. As a rookie detective, he realized that he needed the help of someone who specialized in interpersonal crisis response, and his department hired [[Alina Reardon|Alina]], who was assigned as Layne's partner. The two of them went on to investigate many deeply personal crimes, gaining a key understanding of the human psyche. As they identified culprit after culprit, the pair of them began to build up a bit of a reputation- if a criminal committed a crime and got Layne and Alina involved, they were screwed.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[C.1.4.13.png]] 
> Layne greets Henry, one of Bennett's friends.

While working as partners, Alina gradually fell for Layne and eventually asked him out. Layne agreed, and after a while, they got married, bought a [[Reardon House|house]], and had [[Bennett Reardon|Bennett]] and [[Regan Reardon|Regan]]. They were sure to give their children anything they wanted as they grew up, utilizing their rather large salaries. This support continued into their adulthood after their retirement from the force, with Layne and Alina supporting Bennett as a struggling artist post-grad and Layne trying her best to deal with Regan in her rebellious phase, but things got so ugly with her that he eventually began keeping himself out of it.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[C.1.15.15.png]] 
> Layne and Alina question Bennett and his friends.

When Regan ran away from home, Layne and Alina called up several of their cop friends and started putting their old investigation techniques to work. They spoke with Bennett and his friends [[Jordan Crespo]] and [[Henry Doyle]], attempting to locate the last person Regan had spoken to, but Regan had done too good of a job covering her tracks, and the trail had gone cold.

## Personality
Layne approaches everything in life with a good sense of humor. He loves to joke and act as if nothing is ever serious, and it wasn't in his youth. Layne remained unchanged even when facing no job prospects, and his attitude was an issue early on in the force, but he did good work, and once [[Alina Reardon|Alina]] was partnered with him, their banter and teamwork elevated them into one of the best duos in the area.

As a father, Layne preferred to step back and let Alina handle the difficult conversations, especially those related to [[Regan Reardon|Regan]]. He dealt with [[Bennett Reardon|Bennett]], encouraging him to continue to pursue his dreams of being an artist and helping him entertain his friends. When Regan ran away, Layne was forced to handle a personal matter without humor for the first time ever- it's doable for him, but he dislikes it.
