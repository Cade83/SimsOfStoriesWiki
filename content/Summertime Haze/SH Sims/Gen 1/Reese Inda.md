**Reese Inda** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Summertime Haze]]. She's the girlfriend of [[Antony Jeffers]] and a close friend of [[Hayley Baxter]].

## Profile
Reese was born and raised in [[Oasis Springs]]. In her senior year of high school, she ran and won the position of treasurer in the student government. In that position, she became good friends with [[Hayley Baxter]], the student body president. She watched as Hayley fell for [[Toby Baxter|Toby]], the uber-popular quarterback—especially as the two of them guided the new transfer student, [[Paolo Rocca]], around—and warned him that she had suspicions about Toby's sexuality. Hayley refused to hear it, and while they remained cordial, Reese's suspicions about Toby led to Hayley distancing herself from Reese, especially upon her move to [[Willow Creek]], but they still remained in touch. Toby simply became an unspoken and forbidden topic for the two of them.

After high school, Reese studied to become a teacher and soon had her teaching credentials. She became a substitute teacher, and was regularly called into schools across a wide area, even occasionally working as far off as Willow Creek, where she got to visit with the Baxters. During one visit, while picking up class materials at the [[Willow Creek Archives]], she ran into [[Antony Jeffers]], a librarian, and the two got to talking. Even after she returned home to Oasis Springs, they remained in touch and eventually fell for each other established a long distance relationship, with Reese frequently taking on jobs in Willow Creek.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[C.1.14.13.png]] 
> Reese arrives at the hospital.

It was during one of these visits where she received an urgent call from Toby, telling her that Hayley was in the [[Willow Creek Hospital|hospital]]. Reese rushed to the hospital and agreed to watch over Hayley and his kids, [[Jennifer Baxter|Jennifer]] and [[Nathan Baxter|Nathan]] when asked. As Toby continued to spiral, Reese started working exclusively in Willow Creek and all but moved in with Antony in order to help Toby make ends meet. Reese had no love for Toby, but she valued Hayley deeply, and wanted to make sure her kids were okay.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[C.1.15.8.png]] 
> Reese tells Toby she'd always had her suspicions about him.

Newly connected to the Baxter family, Reese acted as a pseudo-guardian in Hayley's absence, especially with Toby's emotional unavailability. Despite their lack of closeness, Toby clearly saw something in her, as she was the first person he came out since [[Henry Doyle|Henry]], the man who had been Toby's sexual awakening. Reese insisted that Toby tell everything to Hayley as soon as possible. This went poorly, and Reese went to the hospital to try to mediate things between the two of them, but found herself locked out of the room by [[Summer Holiday|a nurse]]. By the time she got in, Hayley had passed.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[C.1.19.24.png]] 
> Reese tells uninvited guests to leave Hayley's funeral.

With Hayley gone, Reese continued to act on her behalf. She helped Toby prepare Hayley's funeral, got outfits for Jennifer and Nathan, and made sure they all got there on time. She found it incredibly frustrating that Toby was more focused on finding Hayley's killer than honoring her memory, and agreed to help him investigate to shut him up for the day. At the funeral, Reese had to eject Henry, [[Jordan Crespo|Jordan]], [[Bennett Reardon|Bennett]], and [[Hope Peck|Hope]], all of whom had attended uninvited. Toby shutdown and was unwilling to eject them himself, even when Reese reminded him that Hayley likely wouldn't have wanted any of them there.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[C.1.22.11.png]] 
> Reese argues with Toby during the questioning.

When an [[Officer|officer]] showed up to interview Reese and Toby as the investigation into Hayley's death began, Reese was less than helpful. She believed that Toby was overly obsessed with Hayley's death, and was becoming increasingly absent as a father. She agreed to continue to assist when Toby asked her to step in as an additional parental figure. The officer identified three suspects and permitted Reese and Toby to sit behind the one-sided glass and observe their questioning. The questioning didn’t unearth any new info outside of all three of the suspects having airtight alibis, and the case went cold.

## Personality
Reese is a very confident woman who places a great deal of trust in her own intuition. It rarely ever fails her, and she's been proven right on multiple occasions, giving her the ability to speak with conviction and without shame in any argument. The biggest of these moments was when [[Toby Baxter|Toby]] revealed his sexuality to her, but this was a bit of a double-edged sword, as Reese also cared a lot about [[Hayley Baxter|Hayley]]'s well being.

Caring too much is what drove Reese to step in for Hayley when she got sick, and what kept her around to look after [[Jennifer Baxter|Jennifer]] and [[Nathan Baxter|Nathan]] even after Hayley passed. She began to see the two of them as children of her own. Similar thoughts were what kept her from moving in with [[Antony Jeffers|Antony]] for the longest time, as she valued her relationships with people in [[Oasis Springs]]. Her sense of caring is rather twisted when it comes to Toby, however, as Reese dislikes him as a person but she does pity him and the fact that his children have to grow up with him as a father.
