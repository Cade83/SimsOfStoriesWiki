**Paolo Rocca** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Summertime Haze]]. He's a friend of [[Toby Baxter|Toby]] and [[Hayley Baxter]].

## Profile
Paolo was born and raised in [[Windenburg]]. As a teenager, he moved out of his parent's house young to party, frequently drinking and dancing in [[Factory One|the house]] he shared with others. This became something that frustrated his parents once his grades began to suffer. At their urging, Paolo woke up one day and realized that he was getting stupider, and was convinced to spend a year studying abroad in [[Oasis Springs]], where he met [[Toby Baxter]] and [[Hayley Baxter|Hayley]], the quarterback of the football team and the student body president, respectively. They guided him around at the school's request, and with time, the three became a trio, with Hayley's friend [[Reese Inda]] as a distant fourth. Paolo mellowed out due to their influence, and even began attending church with the two of them.

He eventually chose to join the football team alongside Toby, sparking a bitter rivalry between the two of them that almost ruined their friendship. After Paolo broke his arm and Toby won an important game in his name, which Paolo cheered on in earnest, everything was forgiven between the two of them. After graduation, Paolo flew back to Windenburg, and after some discussion with his parents, they helped him purchase his old home, which he wanted to rent out for some passive income. He then flew back to Toby and Hayley and joined them when they moved to [[Willow Creek]], wanting to stick by the side of the two people who had changed his life. Toby helped Paolo get hired at the [[Willow Creek Gym]] as a personal trainer, then life settled into a routine for him. His party days were long behind him.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[C.1.12.11.png]] 
> Paolo attends Henry's get together with Toby.

Paolo was invited to attend a get together hosted by a new friend of Toby's, [[Henry Doyle]], also attended by [[Jordan Crespo]], [[Bennett Reardon]], and [[Hope Peck]]. He spoke few words at that get together and the many that came after, but he became beloved by each of them regardless, with Hope in particular having a fascination with his accent and surprising background given his quiet demeanor.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[C.1.20.19.png]] 
> Paolo enjoys lunch with Hope and the rest of Henry's friends.

When Hayley collapsed and ended up in the hospital, Paolo handled her and Toby's children, [[Jennifer Baxter|Jennifer]] and [[Nathan Baxter|Nathan]] until Hayley's longtime friend Reese Inda entered the picture later that day and took over for him. When it came out that Toby had cheated on Hayley with Toby, he completely cut Paolo and the rest of Henry's friend group off, isolating himself with Reese, which was something that bothered Paolo greatly. Unsure of what else to do, he continued spending time with Henry and the others and tried not to address the situation with Toby.

> [!caption|right]
> ![[C.1.19.18.png]] 
> Reese reprimands Paolo at Hayley's funeral.

He wasn't able to reconnect with Toby until Hayley passed, where at Reese's urging, Toby invited Paolo to the funeral under strict instruction from Reese that no one else connected to Henry was welcome. Toby stopped talking to Paolo again following the funeral. Paolo then tried his best to prevent Henry and Toby from reconnecting, fearing further retribution from Reese, but he failed. Luckily for Paolo, Toby reached out to him again and allowed him and Hope to spend time with his family again. 

## Personality
In his youth, Paolo loved to party and lose himself for reasons he didn't quite understand. When he was under the influence of something, he was free of worry, free of concern, and eventually, free of himself. His declining grades were the wakeup call he needed to try to live life, and he went to study abroad and upon meeting [[Toby Baxter|Toby]] and [[Hayley Baxter|Hayley]], he lost much of his former self and habits, even finding value in religion.

Sobriety mellowed Paolo out greatly. He rarely ever contributes to a conversation, as he both doesn't feel a need to and lives a lot of his life in his own head. He's typically conflict averse, but when it comes to Toby, a fire burns within Paolo, shown in their former rivalry on the football team and his concerns about Toby's complicated relationship with [[Henry Doyle|Henry]] hurting Toby.
