**Regan Reardon** is a character introduced in the first chapter of [[Summertime Haze]]. She's the sister of [[Bennett Reardon]].

## Profile
Regan grew up rather privileged as the daughter of [[Alina Reardon|Alina]] and [[Layne Reardon]], who were, in their time, two of the best on [[Willow Creek]]'s police force. Both had larger than life reputations, which followed both Regan and her older brother [[Bennett Reardon|Bennett]] as they grew up. The reputation of Regan's parents weighed on her much heavier than they ever did on Bennett. Bennett, who was several years older than Regan, got to escape for a while when he went off to college, which Regan was stuck at [[Reardon House|home]] with her thoughts, missing Bennett while she continued to chase perfect grades and a sterling reputation. She'd done her best to be the perfect daughter her entire life, but could she ever live up to that concept? 

> [!caption|left]
> ![[C.1.4.16.png]] 
> Regan insults Henry upon meeting him for the first time.

Things began to shift for Regan when  [[Mallory Fuchs]], the infamous bad girl at her high school, took Regan under her wing and convinced her that perfect grades were meaningless and borderline counterintuitive if she wanted to escape her parents' shadow. By the time Bennett returned home from college, Regan had become a girl who lashed out at anyone and everything and did insane, illegal things for the thrill, horrifying Bennett and her parents. She started drinking excessively, then would call Bennett early in the morning and ask him for a ride home, which Bennett eventually started telling her parents about, greatly eroding the trust between the two of them. 

> [!caption|right]
> ![[C.1.12.1.png]] 
> Regan asks if she can join Bennett's friend group.

In an effort to avoid Regan, Bennett began spending a lot more time over at his friend [[Henry Doyle]]'s [[Crespo House|house]], and eventually joined a group Henry formed opposed to [[Ellen Bowers]], who Regan came to understand had a complicated history with Henry. Regan hinted to Bennett that she wanted to be part of it, but Bennett snapped at her and refused to entertain the idea, further wounding Regan. Alina added a greenhouse to their home and made Regan responsible for it, hoping it would teach her responsibility, but this was too little too late. Bennett moved in with Henry not long after, which bothered Regan greatly. She just knew she had to get Bennett back no matter what.

> [!caption|left]
> ![[C.1.15.22.png]] 
> Regan meets with Ellen and [[Jaxon Kauker|Jaxon]].

Regan eventually chose to run away. She planned every last detail utilizing knowledge of missing persons cases she'd heard her parents discuss in her childhood to ensure no one could find her. She crashed at Mallory's [[Mallory and Owain’s|apartment]], which was co-inhabited by her boyfriend, [[Owain Fitzpatrick|Owain]]. While there, she Regan used what she knew about Henry from Bennett to trace him to the [[First Church of Our Mother]], where she learned about all the recent attendees from [[Newcrest]], including Ellen. Regan reached out to Ellen and offered to work with her to free Bennett from Henry, offering Mallory and Owain's assistance as well.
